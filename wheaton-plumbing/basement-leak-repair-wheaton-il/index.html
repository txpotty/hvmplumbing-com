    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Wheaton, IL

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool fast. A dripping T&P valve, a corroded tank bottom or a split washer hose are frequent culprits. We repair or replace the failed part and install drain pans and leak sensors to catch future problems early. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most cases.

    Basement Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Wheaton, IL

    A simple fitting or supply line repair usually costs $200 to $600. Drain stack repairs run $500 to $2,500 depending on access and pipe material. A new sump pump installed typically costs $600 to $1,800, while interior drain channels and full seepage systems can run several thousand dollars.     Interior Drainage Systems in Wheaton

    When exterior excavation isn't feasible, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less invasive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Wheaton, IL

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks usually show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then verify the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Before any repair, our team performs a thorough water damage assessment to identify the source and extent of your basement leak.     Waterproofing for Older Illinois Homes in Wheaton, IL

    Many older homes in Wheaton and across Illinois were not built with modern waterproofing techniques, making them more prone to basement leaks. Newer homes and remodels mostly use copper or PEX for supply lines, while older bungalows often mix several materials. HVM Plumbing specializes in retrofitting older foundations with current solutions, addressing issues like failing mortar, porous concrete, and inadequate drainage.     Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Wheaton

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm typically points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced easily.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Wheaton, IL

    For complete basement leak prevention, exterior waterproofing involves excavating around your foundation and applying waterproof membranes or coatings. This creates a strong barrier against moisture intrusion from the outside. While more involved, this method offers superior long-term protection against water damage, especially for older homes in Wheaton.
