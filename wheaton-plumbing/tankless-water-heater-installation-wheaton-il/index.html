    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Wheaton, IL

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderately hard water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
