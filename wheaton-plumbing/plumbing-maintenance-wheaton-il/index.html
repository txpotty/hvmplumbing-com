    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Wheaton

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Wheaton, IL

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure.     Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Wheaton

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. Much of Illinois sits on heavy clay and rich prairie soil that holds water, which puts pressure on basement walls and drain tile. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Wheaton

    Newer homes and remodels mostly use copper or PEX for supply lines, while older two-flats and bungalows often mix several materials. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Wheaton, IL

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still smart. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Water and sewer lines must be buried well below the frost line to avoid freezing, and shallow lines are a common source of winter trouble. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Wheaton, IL

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.
