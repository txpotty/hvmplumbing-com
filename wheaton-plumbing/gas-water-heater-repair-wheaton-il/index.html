    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Wheaton

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Wheaton

    A worn burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Wheaton home. Proper venting is a critical safety consideration.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Wheaton, IL

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high.     Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Wheaton

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Wheaton, IL

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to inconsistent water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water.
