    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Cicero, IL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Cicero, IL, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Cicero

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Cicero, IL

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. HVM Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Cicero, IL so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Cicero

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Cicero for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Cicero, IL

    Zoeller pumps are known for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Cicero, IL homeowners. HVM Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a quality, long-lasting unit. We can replace your existing Zoeller pump with a comparable model or upgrade you to a more powerful one to meet your needs and local conditions.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Cicero

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes.     Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Cicero

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Cicero
