    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Cicero

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Cicero

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Cicero, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Cicero, IL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Cicero, IL

Our technicians in Cicero, IL use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing endless hot water efficiently.     T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Cicero, IL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
