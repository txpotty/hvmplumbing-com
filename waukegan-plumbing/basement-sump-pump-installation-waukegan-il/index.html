    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Waukegan

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. HVM Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Waukegan home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Waukegan

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not re-enter your Waukegan property.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Waukegan

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Waukegan

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Most Illinois homeowners shut off and drain outdoor spigots in the fall, and many keep cabinet doors open and faucets dripping on the coldest nights. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.
