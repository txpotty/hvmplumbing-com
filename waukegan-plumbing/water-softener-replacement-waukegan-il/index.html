    Metered Softener Replacement in Waukegan

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Waukegan, IL

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem.     Dual Tank Softener Systems in Waukegan

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Waukegan

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not typically required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no major changes to the existing plumbing lines. However, if new lines are run or the system type changes substantially, a permit might be necessary. We handle all needed checks and make sure the job complies with local codes.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or excessive soap use. Other signs are a clear drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    While it may seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional means the job is done correctly, safely and to code.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    New water softeners typically come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from HVM Plumbing.
