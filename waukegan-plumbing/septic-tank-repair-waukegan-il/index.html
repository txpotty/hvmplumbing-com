    Septic Pipe Repair and Replacement in Waukegan, IL

    Damaged pipes leading to or from your septic tank can cause backups, leaks and unpleasant odors. These pipes, often made of PVC or cast iron, can crack due to shifting soil, root invasion or age. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack clay sewer pipes and let tree roots find their way inside.     Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Waukegan

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.
