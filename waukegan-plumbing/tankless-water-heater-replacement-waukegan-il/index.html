    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Waukegan

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Around Waukegan, fast temperature swings in spring and fall can loosen joints and fittings, especially in older homes with mixed pipe materials. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Waukegan

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the fastest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Waukegan, IL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Water and sewer lines must be buried well below the frost line to avoid freezing, and shallow lines are a common source of winter trouble. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
