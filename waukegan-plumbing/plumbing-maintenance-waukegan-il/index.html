    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance.     While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still wise.     Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Waukegan

    Lead service lines remain in many older neighborhoods, and state rules now require utilities to replace them over time.
