    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Chicago, IL

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. HVM Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and provides consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Chicago.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Installation in Chicago

    HVM Plumbing provides complete services for custom shower enclosures, including multi-head showers, body sprays, and steam units. These installations require precise planning for water lines, pressure balancing valves, and drainage systems. We ensure all components are correctly sized and installed to deliver strong performance and water flow, taking into account the water pressure variations sometimes seen in older neighborhoods. Proper sealing is critical for these complex systems.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in Chicago, IL

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures. This includes pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, thermostatic valves for consistent temperature, and different showerhead types like rain showers or handheld units. Our team makes sure all connections are watertight and that the fixtures operate smoothly, improving your showering experience. We can upgrade outdated fixtures to newer, more water-wise models.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Chicago, IL

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Chicago

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Chicago

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can keep up with higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works the way it should.
