    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Chicago, IL

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Water Line Reroute in Chicago

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Cost-Effective Foundation Leak Solutions in Chicago, IL

    We believe in transparent and fair pricing for all our foundation leak repair services in Chicago, IL.     Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Chicago

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Chicago, IL

    The ability to accurately locate a foundation leak is essential for an effective repair. HVM Plumbing invests in modern leak detection technology, including high-frequency listening devices and specialized camera inspections, to pinpoint leaks with precision. This technology allows us to identify the exact location of the break, whether it's a small pinhole or a major burst, minimizing invasive searching and repair costs for our Chicago, IL customers.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Chicago

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Chicago, IL

    Many older homes in Chicago, IL have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Hard water across much of Illinois leaves scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that holds up to pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.
