    Routine Plumbing Maintenance Services in Chicago, IL

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Chicago

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Chicago, IL

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Chicago, IL

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use advanced tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Illinois has a large stock of older homes, and many still have cast iron drains, galvanized steel supply lines, or original clay sewer pipe. Catching these leaks early prevents serious water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Chicago from extensive repairs down the line.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Chicago, IL

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Chicago

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Commercial Plumbing Maintenance in Chicago, IL

    Businesses in Chicago also rely on dependable plumbing systems. Our commercial plumbing maintenance services are suited to the demands of commercial properties, including larger water heaters, multiple restrooms, and specialized equipment. Regular maintenance helps businesses avoid expensive downtime, stay in compliance with health codes, and keep a comfortable environment for employees and customers.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Chicago

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur odors, and grit are frequent reasons homeowners call for treatment or repairs. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Chicago, IL

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still wise. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk in Illinois, especially in exterior walls, unheated garages, and crawlspaces during deep cold snaps. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.
