    Effluent Pump Repair in Chicago, IL

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage comes back into the house.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Chicago

    A clogged drain field is a frequent cause of septic system backups and can be a serious issue for homeowners in Chicago. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future flooding.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Chicago

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Preventative Septic Maintenance & Repair in Chicago

    Many septic tank issues can be prevented with regular maintenance. HVM Plumbing offers routine inspections and preventative repairs to catch small problems before they turn into costly emergencies. This includes checking sludge levels, inspecting pipes for leaks or damage and advising on proper septic system care tailored to the climate and usage patterns common in Chicago.

    Alternative Septic System Repair in Chicago, IL

    Some properties use aerobic treatment units or other alternative systems because of soil or lot size limits. These systems have air pumps, timers and alarms that need regular service. We repair aerators, replace diffusers and troubleshoot control panels. We also help keep maintenance records required by your permit.

    Leach Field Repair in Chicago, IL

    Soggy ground, strong odors or lush grass over the field can point to a struggling disposal area. Causes include clogged distribution pipes, a crushed distribution box or soil sealed by solids. We jet clean lines, replace distribution boxes and determine whether the field can be restored. If replacement is needed, we explain the design and permit steps.
