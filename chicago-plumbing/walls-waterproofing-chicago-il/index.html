    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Chicago

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Basements are the norm across most of Illinois, so sump pumps, floor drains, and wall seepage are common service concerns. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Chicago, IL

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Chicago

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Chicago

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Chicago

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to reduce downtime.     Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Chicago, IL

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.
