    Reliable Water Softener Installation Services in Chicago, IL

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Chicago, IL

    Salt-based water softeners are the most common and effective type, using ion exchange to remove hard minerals like calcium and magnesium. HVM Plumbing specializes in the installation of these traditional systems, making sure of proper brine tank setup and regeneration cycles. We can recommend efficient models that reduce salt and water consumption for your Chicago, IL home.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Chicago

    Modern water softeners offer improved efficiency, using less salt and water during the regeneration process. We install a range of high-efficiency models that can lead to significant savings on your utility bills. These systems are an smart long-term investment for any Chicago homeowner concerned about resource consumption.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Chicago

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Softener Loop Installation in Chicago

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Water Softener Replacement in Chicago, IL

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore full performance.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Chicago

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing beneficial minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an effective option for scale prevention and are popular in many Chicago households.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Chicago, IL

    Pelican Water Systems offer a variety of water treatment solutions, including both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners. If you've chosen a Pelican system for your Chicago, IL home, HVM Plumbing can provide professional installation. Our plumbers make sure of proper sizing, secure connections and correct startup procedures to maximize the effectiveness and lifespan of your Pelican water softener or conditioner.
