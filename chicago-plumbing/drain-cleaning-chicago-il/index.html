    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Chicago

    The cost of drain cleaning in Chicago typically ranges from $150 to $500, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues like main line blockages requiring hydro jetting or camera inspection might cost between $400 and $800. We always provide a written estimate before starting any work.

    Most standard drain cleaning jobs, like clearing a kitchen or bathroom sink, can be finished within 1 to 2 hours. More complex main line blockages or jobs requiring hydro jetting might take 2 to 4 hours, depending on accessibility and severity.

    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs usually take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling noises from pipes, foul odors coming from drains, standing water in sinks or showers, and water backing up into other fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Hydro jetting is recommended for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    For most homes, an annual or twice-yearly professional drain cleaning is sufficient, especially for kitchen and main lines. If you have frequent clogs or an older plumbing system, more frequent cleanings can be helpful to prevent major issues.

    We use professional-grade equipment from well-known brands such as RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their durability and effectiveness in tackling all types of drain blockages, for a thorough job every time.
