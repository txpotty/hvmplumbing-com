    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching.     Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Chicago

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Chicago.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Chicago, IL

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Sectional Point Repair in Chicago

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an effective trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and limits the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Chicago without affecting the entire line.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Chicago, IL

    For badly damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Chicago, IL.

    Pipe Bursting in Chicago, IL

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

Trenchless avoids expenses related to extensive landscaping restoration, driveway replacement, or foundation repairs. For homeowners in Chicago, IL, where traditional digging could damage established gardens or hardscapes, trenchless offers a clear advantage in overall cost and convenience.
