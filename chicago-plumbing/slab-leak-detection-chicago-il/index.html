    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Chicago, IL

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Chicago

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Leak Detection for Older Illinois Homes in Chicago, IL

    Many older homes in Chicago, IL have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more prone to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points. We provide careful analysis for the challenges presented by vintage plumbing under concrete slabs.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Chicago, IL

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Chicago, IL home. We have specific methods for each.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Chicago

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Chicago, IL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Chicago, IL

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We favor non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Chicago, IL from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and lowers the overall cost and time of the repair process.
