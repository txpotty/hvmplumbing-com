    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Chicago

    Many older homes in Chicago still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are vulnerable to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. HVM Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that lowers the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Chicago, IL

    Many older homes in Chicago, IL still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a major upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Chicago, IL

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Chicago, IL

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging.
