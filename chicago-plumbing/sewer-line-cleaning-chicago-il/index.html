    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Chicago

    The cost of sewer line cleaning in Chicago can range from $250 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length of the line, and the method used. More complex issues, like extensive tree root removal or hydro jetting, may be at the higher end. We provide upfront, transparent pricing after a thorough inspection.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets, sewage odors and backups in the lowest drains are common signs.

    In most cases, sewer line cleaning methods like snaking and hydro jetting are non-invasive and do not require digging. We access the line through existing cleanouts. Digging is typically only necessary if there is a severe structural issue like a collapsed pipe requiring replacement, which we would confirm with a camera inspection first.

    For most homes in Chicago, annual or twice-yearly sewer line cleaning is sufficient, especially if you have mature trees nearby or an older plumbing system. If you have frequent clogs or a high-usage commercial property, more frequent cleaning might be advisable to prevent major backups.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go many years without service.

    Drain cleaning usually addresses blockages within individual drain pipes connected to sinks, showers, or toilets. Sewer line cleaning, on the other hand, deals with the main line that carries all wastewater from your home to the municipal sewer system or septic tank. A sewer line issue affects multiple fixtures.

    Rental machines can work on simple clogs, but they can kink or get stuck on roots. A pro also offers a camera to confirm the line is clear.

    We strongly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs.
