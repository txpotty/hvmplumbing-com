    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Chicago, IL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Chicago

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. A long heating season keeps boilers and water heaters running hard, and summer humidity can cause sweating pipes and damp basements. Properly working components are essential for both efficiency and safety.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Chicago

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Chicago

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Chicago, IL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
