    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Chicago, IL

    For added protection, many homeowners in Chicago choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. HVM Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Chicago

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Chicago home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Chicago

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Chicago, IL

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a smart habit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Chicago

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Chicago for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Chicago, IL

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. HVM Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Chicago, IL so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is important for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Chicago

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.
