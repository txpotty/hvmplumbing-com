    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Chicago

    Most well pressure tank replacements cost $900 to $2,200 installed, depending on tank size and whether the switch and tank tee are replaced. Large capacity or multi-tank setups can run $2,500 or more. We give a firm price before starting.

    A typical well pressure tank replacement generally takes between 2 to 4 hours. This includes disconnecting the old tank, installing the new one, and testing the system to ensure proper operation and pressure settings.

    Well pressure tank replacement involves working with pressurized water systems, electrical components, and proper sizing, which can be complex and dangerous for untrained individuals. We highly recommend hiring a licensed professional to ensure safety, code compliance, and proper system function.

    If your switch is more than a few years old or the contacts are pitted, replacing it with the tank is inexpensive insurance. It also lets us match the switch settings to the new tank.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, completely isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm. Bladder tanks are often preferred for their longevity and ease of maintenance.

    A like-for-like tank replacement usually does not require a permit in most counties. Electrical changes or new piping may, and we check local rules for you.

    On average, a well pressure tank can last between 5 to 10 years, though some high-quality tanks can last longer. Factors like water quality, proper initial installation, and regular maintenance can significantly impact its lifespan.

    Most pressure tanks last 10 to 15 years. Hard water, sand and frequent cycling can shorten that, especially when the pre-charge is never checked.
