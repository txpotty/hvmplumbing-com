    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Chicago

    The cost of garbage disposal replacement in Chicago, IL typically ranges from $200 to $500, including the new unit and labor. This can vary based on the model chosen, its horsepower, and any necessary plumbing modifications. We provide a detailed quote upfront.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is recommended.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    Leaks from the body, frequent resets, constant jams, loud grinding and age over ten years are common signs.

    We replace all major brands of garbage disposals, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, GE, KitchenAid, and Whirlaway. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements and features of each brand, ensuring a proper and efficient replacement every time.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually enough. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your Chicago home.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can cut that life.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running.
