    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Champaign

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Champaign

    Backwater valves, overhead sewers, and battery backup sump pumps are popular upgrades for homes with a history of flooding. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Champaign

    HVM Plumbing is proficient in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, ensuring the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for efficient repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Champaign, IL

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Champaign

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, avoiding breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Champaign property.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Champaign

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Champaign residence.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Champaign

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.
