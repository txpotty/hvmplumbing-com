    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Champaign

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Champaign

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Champaign, IL

    For high-hazard applications in Champaign, IL where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HVM Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Champaign, IL

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Champaign, IL, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Champaign

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Champaign, IL usually ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is generally on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial systems or installations requiring significant pipe changes might take longer. We always aim to complete the work efficiently while keeping our quality standards high.

    Constant dripping or discharge from an RPZ relief valve, low pressure downstream and a failed annual test are typical signs. Visible corrosion or a cracked body from freezing also means service is needed.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then annually. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.

    We install all common types of backflow preventers, including reduced pressure zone assemblies, pressure vacuum breakers and double check valve assemblies. Our team will recommend the right device based on your property's hazard level and local plumbing codes in Champaign.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.
