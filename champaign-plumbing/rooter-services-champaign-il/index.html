    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Champaign

    The cost of rooter services in Champaign, IL can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 to $500 for standard drain snaking, depending on the severity and location of the clog.     Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and dispose of fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash.     Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and usually more effective.
