    Preventing Water Heater Damage in Champaign

    Thermal expansion is a common cause of premature water heater failure in Champaign homes. Without an expansion tank, constant pressure swings stress the water heater's tank, connections and internal components, leading to leaks, reduced efficiency and a shorter lifespan. Our installation service solves this issue, providing a buffer that protects your investment. This small addition can save you thousands in future water heater repair or replacement costs.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Champaign, IL

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.
