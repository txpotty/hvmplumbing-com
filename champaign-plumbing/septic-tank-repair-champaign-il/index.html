    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Champaign

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Septic Inlet Line Repair in Champaign

    The line from your house to the tank can crack, sag or separate from soil movement and roots. Symptoms include gurgling drains and backups even after pumping. We camera the line to locate the problem and repair or replace the damaged section. A cleanout near the tank makes future maintenance simpler.
