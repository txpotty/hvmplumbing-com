    Mold prevention is a key part of our process. We achieve this through rapid water extraction, thorough drying with air movers and dehumidifiers, and applying antimicrobial treatments to affected areas. Our moisture monitoring confirms all hidden moisture is removed before mold can take hold.

    Signs of hidden water damage include musty odors, visible mold growth on walls or ceilings, peeling paint or wallpaper, warped flooring, and stained drywall.     The timeline for water damage restoration typically ranges from 3 to 7 days for drying and initial cleanup, but it can extend to several weeks or even months if extensive repairs and reconstruction are needed. Factors like the amount of water, humidity levels, and materials involved all influence the total duration. We focus on efficient service to get your home back to normal quickly.

    Many policies cover sudden and accidental damage, such as a burst pipe or failed water heater. Gradual leaks, poor maintenance and flooding from outside water are often excluded. Check your policy and contact your insurer right away.
