    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Glenview

    The cost of trenchless sewer line repair in Glenview, IL can vary widely, typically ranging from $4,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the length of the pipe, the method used and the complexity of the job. Factors like pipe diameter and accessibility also play a role, so a professional assessment is key for an accurate estimate.

    Lines that are cracked, root-infested or corroded but still hold their shape are typically good candidates for lining. Collapsed or badly offset lines may need pipe bursting or a spot excavation.

    A properly installed liner has no joints for roots to enter, so root intrusion is essentially eliminated in the lined section.

    Trenchless repair is very versatile and effective for issues like cracks, fractures, root intrusion and minor collapses. If the pipe has completely disintegrated, has severe offsets or a major belly, traditional excavation might be a more suitable option. A video inspection will determine the right approach.

    A rented snake may temporarily clear a clog, but it will not fix cracks, offsets or roots. Repeated DIY clearing can also damage fragile pipe, so a camera inspection is the better first step.

    Common signs include recurring drain backups, foul odors coming from drains, gurgling noises from toilets or drains and lush patches of grass in your yard that can point to a leak. If you notice any of these in your Glenview home, it is time for a professional inspection.
