    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Glenview, IL

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Glenview, IL

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Glenview, IL

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Glenview, IL.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Glenview

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Glenview, IL

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system.
