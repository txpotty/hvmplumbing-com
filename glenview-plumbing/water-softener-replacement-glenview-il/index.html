    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Glenview, IL

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Glenview

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Fleck Water Softener Replacement in Glenview, IL

    Fleck is another leading brand known for durable water softener components. When your Fleck system needs replacing in Glenview, IL, our plumbers are proficient with these units. We will carefully remove the old Fleck valve and tanks, install the new equipment and program it to your water hardness and usage patterns. That restores efficient water softening.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Glenview

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Glenview

    Restaurants, salons and small businesses rely on soft water to protect dishwashers, espresso machines and water heaters. We replace commercial softeners sized to flow rate and daily usage. Larger valves and multiple tanks may be needed to keep up with demand. We schedule installs to minimize downtime. We can also add filtration ahead of the softener if needed.
