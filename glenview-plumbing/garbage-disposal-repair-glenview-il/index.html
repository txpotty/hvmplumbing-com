    Leaking Disposal Repair in Glenview, IL

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the better option.

    Noisy Garbage Disposal Repair in Glenview

    Unusual noises from your garbage disposal, such as grinding, rattling, or humming, point to internal issues. This could be a loose impeller, a foreign object caught inside, or even motor bearing problems. Our technicians pinpoint the source of the noise and perform the necessary repairs to restore quiet operation to your kitchen in Glenview.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Glenview

    A failed disposal can back water into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or leaking onto the floor, turn off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us. We respond quickly to stop the backup and repair or replace the unit. That helps prevent damage to cabinets and floors.

    Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Glenview

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a less noisy replacement unit.

    Electrical Issues & Motor Repair in Glenview

    If your garbage disposal hums but doesn't grind, or doesn't turn on at all, it could be an electrical issue or a motor problem. We check circuit breakers, reset buttons, and internal wiring. For motor failures, we assess if a repair is feasible or if replacement is the more cost-effective solution, always keeping your safety first in Glenview.

    Unusual Noises and Odor Diagnosis in Glenview

    Strange grinding, clanking, or humming noises, along with persistent foul odors, are clear signs your garbage disposal needs attention.
