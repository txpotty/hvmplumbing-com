    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Peoria

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Peoria, IL typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can affect the final price.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it carefully. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration a bit.

    We install InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen and GE. We help you pick based on power, noise and warranty.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually sufficient. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you weigh your needs and choose the best fit for your Peoria home.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms usually indicate internal motor or component failure.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can help.

    In most cases, a direct replacement of a garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a plumbing permit in Peoria, IL. However, if there are significant modifications to your kitchen plumbing or electrical system involved, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers are knowledgeable about local codes and will advise you if any permits are needed.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is the better choice.
