    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Peoria

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Peoria.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Peoria

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Peoria

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Peoria, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Peoria

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or excessive energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Around Peoria, fast temperature swings in spring and fall can stress joints and fittings, especially in older homes with mixed pipe materials. Properly working components are essential for both efficiency and safety.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Peoria

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Peoria, IL

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Peoria, IL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
