    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Peoria

    The cost for professional slab leak detection in Peoria, IL usually ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for diagnosis. This initial detection fee is separate from any repair costs, which can vary a lot based on the leak's location and required pipe repair.

    For major slab leak repairs, especially those involving excavation or extensive re-piping, a plumbing permit from the local building department is often required. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes so your repair is compliant with local codes and regulations, protecting your home.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Illinois homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a strong indicator.

    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a fast diagnosis so repair work can begin promptly.

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes vulnerable to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which usually causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Many policies cover the cost to access the leak and repair resulting water damage, though the pipe itself may not be covered. Coverage varies by insurer. We provide photos and a written report for your claim.
