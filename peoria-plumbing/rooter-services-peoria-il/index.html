    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Peoria

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for efficient service with lasting results.

    HVM Plumbing stands by the quality of our work. We offer a warranty on our rooter services, the details of which will be provided with your estimate. This gives you peace of mind knowing that our solutions are designed to last.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is clean after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, careful service.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at HVM Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes. Our technicians are skilled in operating the equipment correctly to clear blockages without causing damage. However, if pipes are already weakened, such as corroded or cracked, some methods may worsen existing issues, which is why a camera inspection is often recommended first.
