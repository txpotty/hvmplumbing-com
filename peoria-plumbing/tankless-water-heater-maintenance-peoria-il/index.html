    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Peoria

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Peoria, IL generally ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required. This includes a full flush and inspection of critical components. More complex issues or additional repairs would be quoted on their own.

    Yes, regular maintenance is key for maintaining your tankless water heater's energy efficiency. Descaling removes mineral deposits that force the unit to work harder, using more energy to heat water. Clean components and proper calibration help your unit operates at its designed efficiency, saving you money on energy bills.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, restoring heat transfer efficiency.

    A typical tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, generally takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and whether any minor adjustments or repairs are needed during the visit.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in under half that time.

    If you have isolation valves, a pump and the right solution, a handy homeowner can do it. A pro visit also checks gas pressure, venting, sensors and condensate, which a DIY flush skips.
