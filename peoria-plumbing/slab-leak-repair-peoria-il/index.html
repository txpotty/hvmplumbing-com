    Dependable Slab Leak Repair Services in Peoria, IL

    Slab Leak Reroute in Peoria

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Peoria

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Peoria with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Slab Leak Detection in Peoria

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to verify a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Peoria, IL

    Whether your home in Peoria, IL has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, ensuring a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Peoria, IL

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Peoria

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Peoria, IL

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Peoria, IL.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Peoria

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools. Our plumbers use sensitive electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precise detection minimizes the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, for a complete diagnosis of your Peoria home.
