    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Peoria

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Peoria

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. HVM Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, preventing future issues in the same spot.

    PEX Repiping in Peoria

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Peoria

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Most Illinois homeowners shut off and drain outdoor spigots in the fall, and many keep cabinet doors open and faucets dripping on the coldest nights. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. HVM Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Peoria, IL

    Many older homes in Peoria, IL still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, clear water throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Peoria

    Slab leaks, where pipes under your concrete foundation leak, are a serious concern for homeowners in Peoria. These leaks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and wasted water. HVM Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to pinpoint the exact location of the leak.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Peoria, IL

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.
