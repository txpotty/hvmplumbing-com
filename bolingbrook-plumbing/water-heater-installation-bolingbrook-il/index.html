    Electric Water Heater Installation in Bolingbrook, IL

    Electric water heaters are common in homes without natural gas access or where homeowners prefer electric appliances. Our installation process for electric units in Bolingbrook includes verifying correct electrical wiring, breaker sizing and secure connections. While electric units can have higher operating costs than gas in some areas, they are often simpler to install and maintain. We'll help you select an efficient model that fits your budget and needs.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Bolingbrook, IL

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Bolingbrook, IL

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Bolingbrook

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a solid choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Bolingbrook, IL

    Tank water heater installations usually cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting. We also compare long-term energy costs so you can make an educated choice.
