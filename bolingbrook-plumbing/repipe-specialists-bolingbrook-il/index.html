    Many older homes in Bolingbrook, IL still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup.     PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Bolingbrook

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Bolingbrook

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack older sewer laterals and let tree roots find their way inside. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Bolingbrook, IL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Bolingbrook, IL. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
