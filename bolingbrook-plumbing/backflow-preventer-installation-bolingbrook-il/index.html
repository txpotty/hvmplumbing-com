    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Bolingbrook, IL

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Bolingbrook, IL, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Bolingbrook

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Bolingbrook. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HVM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Bolingbrook

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.
