    Control Valve and Injector Service in Bolingbrook, IL

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Bolingbrook

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Bolingbrook, IL typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the system's condition and the services required. Factors like cleaning a heavily fouled brine tank or replacing minor worn parts can influence the final price. We provide a detailed quote before starting any work.

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur odors, and grit are frequent reasons homeowners call for treatment or repairs. Hard water carries minerals like calcium and magnesium, and regular water softener maintenance is crucial to prevent scale buildup in pipes and appliances. A well-kept system keeps fighting those minerals and safeguards your home's plumbing.

    High-purity evaporated salt pellets cause fewer bridges and less sludge than rock salt. Potassium chloride is an option but may need stronger settings.

    Most visits take about an hour, including testing and a manual regeneration. Resin replacement can add another hour or so.

    Maintenance typically includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to verify effectiveness.

    A brine tank full of water usually means a clogged injector, a stuck float or a drain line problem. Our service clears the cause and resets the system.
