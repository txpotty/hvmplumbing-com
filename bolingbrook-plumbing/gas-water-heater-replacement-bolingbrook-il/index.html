    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure.     Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Bolingbrook, IL

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Bolingbrook
