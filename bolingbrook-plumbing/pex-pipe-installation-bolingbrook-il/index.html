    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Bolingbrook

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. A long heating season keeps boilers and water heaters running hard, and summer humidity can cause sweating pipes and damp basements. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, significantly reducing the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Bolingbrook.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers commonly offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    We install high-quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other proven brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Bolingbrook homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or tight attic access can take a bit longer.

    We primarily use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Illinois plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.
