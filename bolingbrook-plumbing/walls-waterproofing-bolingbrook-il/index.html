    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Bolingbrook

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Bolingbrook, IL can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump typically fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide transparent, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies a lot. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week. We'll provide a clear timeline during your first visit.

    Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems specifically engineered to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is essential.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Skilled DIYers can roll on liquid membranes, but mistakes at seams, corners and drains often lead to leaks. Shower pans also require correct slope and flood testing. Professional installation lowers the risk of hidden water damage.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty in force.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is the smart choice.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, complete walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to poor results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.
