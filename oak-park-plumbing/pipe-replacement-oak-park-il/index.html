    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Oak Park, IL

    Many older homes in Oak Park, IL still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to rust and corrosion on the inside. This leads to reduced water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. HVM Plumbing provides complete galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also shields your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Oak Park, IL

    Most Illinois homeowners shut off and drain outdoor spigots in the fall, and many keep cabinet doors open and faucets dripping on the coldest nights. Cold snaps can lead to burst pipes, especially in exposed areas or unheated spaces. When a pipe bursts in your Oak Park, IL home, it causes instant water damage and requires urgent attention. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences. We work quickly to minimize disruption and water loss.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Oak Park, IL

    Deciding between pipe repair and full replacement depends on several factors, including the age of your pipes, the extent of the damage, and the frequency of past issues. A small, isolated leak might be repaired, but widespread corrosion or multiple leaks typically warrant replacement. HVM Plumbing provides honest assessments in Oak Park, IL, explaining the pros and cons of each option. We help you make an informed decision that offers the most cost-effective and long-lasting solution for your home.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Oak Park, IL

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Oak Park

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. HVM Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, preventing future issues in the same spot.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Oak Park

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Oak Park, IL

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.
