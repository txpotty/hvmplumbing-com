    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Oak Park

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack older sewer laterals and let tree roots find their way inside. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
