    Slab leaks can cause significant damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels.     Basements are the norm across most of Illinois, so sump pumps, floor drains, and foundation cracks are common service concerns. Hard water can also corrode copper pipes from the inside.
