    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Des Plaines

    The cost of a whole-home repipe in Des Plaines varies a great deal depending on the size of your home, the number of fixtures, and the chosen material (PEX or copper). Generally, you can expect to pay between $4,000 and $15,000 for a PEX repipe, and $8,000 to $25,000 or more for copper. We provide a detailed, itemized quote after an on-site assessment.

    Most whole-home repipes can be completed within 3 to 5 days, depending on the size and complexity of your home and the chosen piping material. We strive to limit water shut-off periods, often restoring water service by the end of each workday. Our team works efficiently to get your plumbing fully operational as soon as possible.

    Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Illinois, each with distinct advantages. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally less expensive, making it a great option for many homes. Copper is highly durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur odors, and grit are frequent reasons homeowners call for treatment or repairs. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup restricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can reduce these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    While fixing individual leaks might seem cheaper in the short term, it's often a temporary solution for an aging plumbing system. If you're experiencing multiple leaks, low pressure, or discolored water, it indicates widespread pipe wear. Repiping addresses the root cause by replacing the entire system, providing a long-term, dependable solution and preventing future problems.

    We primarily install high-quality copper piping from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro, known for their durability and purity. For PEX systems, we often use leading brands such as Uponor (Wirsbo Aquapex), Zurn PEX, or Viega PEX, chosen for their proven reliability and performance in homes.

    While some minor wall and ceiling access is usually necessary for a repipe, our repipe specialists use techniques that minimize damage. We strategically cut access points and patch them neatly after the new pipes are installed. We aim to keep disruptions to your home's finishes as minimal as possible during the process.
