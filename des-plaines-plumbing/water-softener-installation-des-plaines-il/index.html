    Softener Loop Installation in Des Plaines

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Des Plaines

    Accurate testing is key for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Des Plaines

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Des Plaines home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including necessary bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide complete protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Des Plaines

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing beneficial minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an excellent option for scale prevention and are popular in many Des Plaines households.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Des Plaines

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. HVM Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Des Plaines home.
