    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Des Plaines

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides crucial protection when the power goes out. We install reliable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an essential safeguard for homes in Des Plaines that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We tie these systems cleanly with your existing plumbing.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Des Plaines

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
