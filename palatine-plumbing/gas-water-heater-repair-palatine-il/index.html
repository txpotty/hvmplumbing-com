    Pilot Light Repair in Palatine

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Palatine, IL

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Palatine

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. Our technicians in Palatine are adept at diagnosing these components and performing the needed repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts.     Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Palatine, IL
