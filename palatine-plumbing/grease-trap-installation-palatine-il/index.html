    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Palatine

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water.     Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Palatine, IL

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Illinois ordinances for waste management.
