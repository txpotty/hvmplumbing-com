    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Palatine, IL

    Pipe Bursting in Palatine, IL

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Lining reduces the inside diameter slightly, but the smooth surface often improves flow. Pipe bursting can maintain or increase the diameter.

    A properly installed epoxy liner is designed to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.

    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, which leaves you with a completely new pipe.

    Trenchless pipe repairs, especially CIPP lining and pipe bursting using materials like epoxy resins and HDPE pipe, are designed to last for 50 years or more. These materials are highly resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and chemical breakdown, offering a very durable solution.
