    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Palatine

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Palatine

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in Palatine usually costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    HVM Plumbing services all major brands of water heaters, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, State, Kenmore, Noritz, Rinnai, and more. Our technicians are trained on a broad range of models and systems.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that accumulate at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.

    Many homeowners can do a basic flush, but older drain valves can break or leak. A pro also tests safety valves, venting and gas lines.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are key for extending the lifespan of your water heater, especially where water quality varies.

    Generally, a permit is not required for routine water heater maintenance, since it involves inspections and minor adjustments rather than new installations or major repairs. If a repair during maintenance becomes substantial, we will advise on any necessary permits.
