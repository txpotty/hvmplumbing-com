    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Palatine

    The cost of drain cleaning in Palatine can vary a great deal based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after inspecting the issue.

    Avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets. Regular preventative maintenance, like an annual professional drain cleaning, can also be very effective.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer and more effective.

    Hydro jetting is a thorough drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs, tree root intrusions, grease buildup, or when traditional snaking isn't effective, particularly for main sewer lines.

    Most single fixture drain cleanings take under an hour. Main line cleaning and hydro jetting usually take one to three hours.

    Tree roots are naturally attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines, and dry spells can make them seek out any available water source. Tiny cracks or loose joints in older sewer pipes provide an entry point, allowing roots to grow and eventually block the line. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack older sewer laterals and let tree roots find their way inside. Shifting soil can also cause pipe movement, creating these access points.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a sag in the pipe.
