    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Hoffman Estates, IL

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Hoffman Estates, IL

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Hoffman Estates, IL.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Hoffman Estates

    Heavy spring and summer storms often overwhelm older sewer systems in Illinois, causing sewage backups through floor drains. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.
