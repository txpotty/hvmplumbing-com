    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Hoffman Estates

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clean.

    Commercial Drain Clog Clearing in Hoffman Estates, IL

    Restaurants, salons and offices see heavy drain use and need fast, dependable service. We clear grease-heavy kitchen lines, hair-clogged salon sinks and mop sinks. Preventive jetting schedules are available to keep lines open between visits. We can work early or late to avoid interrupting your business.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Hoffman Estates, IL

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we inspect both.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Hoffman Estates, IL

    For severe or recurring clogs, hydro-jetting is an excellent solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away buildup, grease, scale, and even tree roots from your pipes. It's a thorough cleaning process that leaves pipes scoured, reducing the likelihood of future blockages and extending pipe life, especially in homes with hard water deposits.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Hoffman Estates

    Beyond clearing existing clogs, HVM Plumbing also provides preventative drain maintenance services. Regular cleaning, especially for homes dealing with mineral buildup from hard water, can greatly reduce the risk of future blockages. We can recommend and apply professional-grade drain treatments and schedule routine inspections to keep your plumbing system in top condition.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Hoffman Estates

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and commercial kitchens collect dirt, sand and debris, especially after storms track grit indoors. When their traps dry out from sitting unused, sewer gas can also escape. We clear the drain, flush the trap and can install trap primers or trap seals to keep odors out.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Hoffman Estates, IL

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces while we are there.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Hoffman Estates, IL

    A clogged main sewer line can affect all drains in your home and is a serious issue. Causes often include tree root intrusion, pipe collapse, or significant grease buildup, common in older homes. We use advanced camera inspections to locate the blockage and hydro-jetting to thoroughly clear it, restoring full drainage to your entire property.
