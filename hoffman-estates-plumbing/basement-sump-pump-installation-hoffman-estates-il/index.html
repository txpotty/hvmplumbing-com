    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Hoffman Estates

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes.     Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Hoffman Estates

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab generally falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain. If your pump is more than 7 to 10 years old, it is also smart to consider a replacement before it fails completely.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also warning signs.

    Replacing a pump generally does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they are needed.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, built to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for maximum protection.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely within the sump pit, making it quieter and less obtrusive. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable. Both types are effective for removing water from your basement.

    Plumbers in Illinois must hold a state license, so confirming a license before work starts is one of the easiest ways to protect your home. In many areas, a plumbing permit is required for new sump pump installations or significant replacements so the work meets local building codes. HVM Plumbing is familiar with local permitting requirements in Hoffman Estates and can pull the necessary permits so your installation is compliant.

    A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unforeseen challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to limit disruption to your home.
