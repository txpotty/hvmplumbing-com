    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Hoffman Estates

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an effective solution.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Hoffman Estates

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Hoffman Estates can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Hoffman Estates

    PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it well suited for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Hoffman Estates, PEX is the pipe to use.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Hoffman Estates

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Hard water across much of Illinois leaves mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Hoffman Estates

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can drastically improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and eliminates the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Hoffman Estates.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Hoffman Estates, IL

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are often included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options together.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Hoffman Estates

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get properly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule on track.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Hoffman Estates, IL

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when finished. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is needed.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing.
