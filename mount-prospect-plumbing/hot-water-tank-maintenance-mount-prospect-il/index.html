    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Mount Prospect

    The cost of hot water tank maintenance in Mount Prospect, IL typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required. This usually includes flushing, anode rod inspection and safety valve checks. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a little longer.

    Many homeowners can do a basic drain with a garden hose. The risk is a plastic drain valve that breaks or will not reseal, and DIY flushes seldom include an anode check or relief valve test.

    That smell typically comes from bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. We can switch to an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank to get rid of it.

    Our technicians are trained to service all major hot water tank brands, including Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, State, Kenmore and more. We have the experience to handle various models, whether gas or electric.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records protects your warranty.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining starts to rust.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.
