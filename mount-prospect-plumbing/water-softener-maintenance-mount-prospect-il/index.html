    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Mount Prospect, IL

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Mount Prospect homes.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Mount Prospect, IL

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Mount Prospect

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Mount Prospect.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Mount Prospect

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we analyze the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to steady soft water throughout your home in Mount Prospect.
