    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Mount Prospect, IL

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Mount Prospect

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Mount Prospect homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Mount Prospect, IL

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Mount Prospect

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.
