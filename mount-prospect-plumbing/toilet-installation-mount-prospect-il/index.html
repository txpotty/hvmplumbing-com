    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Mount Prospect

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    HVM Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style tastes, and water efficiency goals.

    HVM Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works correctly. Specific warranty details will be included with your service agreement.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are easy to find and perform well.

    Most standard toilet installations can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as wall-mounted or smart toilets, may take longer due to additional plumbing or electrical work.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location usually does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, typically around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's usually best left to professionals.
