    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Normal

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in Normal usually costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    Generally, a permit is not required for routine water heater maintenance, since it involves inspections and minor adjustments rather than new installations or major repairs. If a repair during maintenance becomes substantial, we will explain any necessary permits.

    Common signs include strange noises like rumbling or popping, rusty or discolored hot water, a rotten egg smell, reduced hot water supply, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. If you notice any of these, it's time for a professional check.

    Many manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep warranties valid, especially for tankless units. We provide service records for your records.

    HVM Plumbing stands behind all our services with a robust workmanship warranty. We make sure our maintenance is performed correctly and effectively. Specific warranty details will be provided when service is complete.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that collect at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.

    Many homeowners can do a basic flush, but older drain valves can break or leak. A pro also tests safety valves, venting and gas lines.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are crucial for extending the lifespan of your water heater, particularly where water quality varies.
