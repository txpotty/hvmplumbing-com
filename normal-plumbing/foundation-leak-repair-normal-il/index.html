    Foundation Leak Detection in Normal

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Normal, IL

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Normal

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Normal, IL

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. HVM Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Normal, IL home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Normal, IL

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to locate cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We walk you through the trade-offs of each method.

    Water Line Reroute in Normal

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Normal, IL

    For homes with basements in Normal, IL, wall leaks can be a significant concern. We offer complete basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, sealing cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures prevent water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.
