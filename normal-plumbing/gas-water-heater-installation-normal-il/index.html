    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Normal

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the smarter option.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and ensure correct setup for your fuel source.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 and 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or major gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work quickly to minimize disruption to your home.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for gas water heater installation. We pull permits when required and make sure the work passes inspection.
