    Preventative Leak Detection in Normal, IL

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use modern tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Newer homes and remodels mostly use copper or PEX for supply lines, while older bungalows often mix several materials. Catching these leaks early prevents serious water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Normal from extensive repairs down the line.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Normal

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Normal

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack older sewer laterals and let tree roots find their way inside. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Normal, IL

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Normal

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Normal

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Normal

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Hard water across much of Illinois leaves scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.
