    Steam Shower Plumbing in Normal

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.

    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Normal, IL

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. HVM Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and provides consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Normal.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Normal

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can handle higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works as intended.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Normal, IL
