    PEX Water Line Repair in Normal, IL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option increasingly used in homes across Normal, IL. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make solid, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Normal

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Normal

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are skilled in repairing both. PEX offers flexibility and corrosion resistance, while copper is known for its durability. We carry a full stock of appropriate fittings and tools for each material, ensuring a proper, code-compliant repair. Our expertise extends to identifying common failure points for each type, such as fitting issues with PEX or pinhole leaks in older copper lines, frequent in homes with harder water.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Normal

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Lead service lines remain in many older neighborhoods, and state rules now call for utilities to replace them over time. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Normal is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Normal, IL

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Normal, IL

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderate water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Normal

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Normal looking to protect their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.
