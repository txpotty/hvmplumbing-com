    Sewer lines can suffer from many forms of damage, including cracks, breaks, corrosion and sagging sections due to shifting soil.     Post-Clearing Sewer Camera Inspection in Normal

    After a backup is cleared, a camera inspection reveals why it happened. It might be grease, roots, a broken section or an object stuck in the line. Knowing the cause helps you avoid repeat backups. We often recommend an inspection after any main line cleaning.

    Sewer Line Locating and Depth Marking in Normal, IL

    Before any repair, it helps to know precisely where your sewer line runs and how deep it is. Our camera includes a sonde transmitter that we trace with a receiver above ground.     Root Intrusion Detection in Normal, IL

    Tree roots are a common problem for sewer lines, especially with the mature trees found in many Normal neighborhoods. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate even tiny cracks in pipes, growing and eventually causing severe blockages or pipe damage. Our high-resolution cameras clearly show root masses, allowing us to recommend appropriate solutions like hydro-jetting or pipe repair. We use professional locators to mark the exact spot above ground.

    Belly and Offset Detection in Normal, IL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Basements are the norm across most of Illinois, so sump pumps, floor drains, and foundation cracks are common service concerns.
