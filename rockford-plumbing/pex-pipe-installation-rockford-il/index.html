    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Rockford

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Rockford can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Rockford, IL

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Rockford

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Rockford, IL varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. Generally, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, clear estimates before starting any work.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your Rockford home.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or difficult attic access can take a bit longer.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and rejected inspections.

    Whole-home repipes typically require a plumbing permit and inspection. We handle the permit process as part of the job.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Illinois plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.
