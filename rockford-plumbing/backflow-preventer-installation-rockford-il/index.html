    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Rockford

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Rockford, IL

    For high-hazard applications in Rockford, IL where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HVM Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Rockford

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Rockford. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HVM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Rockford

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification.
