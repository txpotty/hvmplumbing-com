    Jet Pump System Repair in Rockford, IL

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HVM Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Rockford operates at peak efficiency.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Rockford

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.
