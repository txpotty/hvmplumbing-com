    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Rockford

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Rockford with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Rockford, IL

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the damaged section. Our technicians are skilled in careful concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is properly restored.     Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Rockford, IL

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Rockford

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Rockford with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Rockford, IL

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Rockford home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.
