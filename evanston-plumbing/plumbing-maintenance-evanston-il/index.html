    Essential Plumbing Maintenance Services in Evanston, IL

    Water Heater Maintenance in Evanston

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Hard water across much of Illinois leaves mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Evanston

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Evanston, IL

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Evanston

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Evanston

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Evanston

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Evanston

    Illinois has a large stock of older homes, and many still have cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, or original clay sewer pipe. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Evanston

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, identifying potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Much of Illinois sits on heavy clay and rich prairie soil that holds water, which puts pressure on basement walls and drain tile. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and costly emergency repairs.
