    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Evanston

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderately hard water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
