    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Evanston

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Evanston, IL

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Illinois has cold winters and hot, humid summers, and those seasonal extremes put steady stress on pipes, water heaters, and sump pumps. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Evanston, IL basement.

Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Evanston, IL properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.
