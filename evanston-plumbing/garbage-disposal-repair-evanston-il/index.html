    Electrical Issues & Motor Repair in Evanston

    If your garbage disposal hums but doesn't grind, or doesn't turn on at all, it could be an electrical issue or a motor problem. We check circuit breakers, reset buttons, and internal wiring.     Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Evanston

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring. Air switches and batch-feed lids are tested too. Most electrical issues are easy to fix once found.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Evanston

    A humming disposal that will not spin usually has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button when needed.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Evanston

    A failed disposal can back water into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or leaking onto the floor, turn off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us. We respond quickly to stop the backup and repair or replace the unit. That helps prevent damage to cabinets and floors.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Evanston, IL

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes when needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the better option.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Evanston, IL

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly usually has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what to keep out of the disposal.
