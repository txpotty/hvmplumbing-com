    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Evanston, IL

    When repairs are no longer enough due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer several replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive solution for your Illinois property, ensuring a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Evanston

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Evanston, IL

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to significant damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. HVM Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures. We precisely locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to limit disruption.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Evanston

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Evanston

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Illinois properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Evanston.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Evanston

    Before any repair, we use high-resolution video camera inspection to precisely locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This no-dig method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a precise diagnosis and repair plan. It's key for understanding issues in older homes in Evanston and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Evanston, IL

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Evanston.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Evanston

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.
