    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Evanston, IL

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Evanston, IL home. We have specific methods for each.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Evanston

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Evanston homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Commercial Slab Leak Detection in Evanston, IL

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Evanston, IL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Evanston, IL

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We prioritize non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Evanston, IL from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and lowers the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Leak Detection for Older Illinois Homes in Evanston, IL

    Many older homes in Evanston, IL have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more susceptible to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points.     Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Evanston, IL

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as it can be.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Evanston, IL

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.
