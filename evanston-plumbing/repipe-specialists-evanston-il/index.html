    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Evanston

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Evanston. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Evanston

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Evanston, IL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. HVM Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Evanston, IL, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Evanston

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more complex. We coordinate with HOAs, schedule water shutoffs and protect neighboring units. For property managers we offer building-wide repipes done in phases to keep residents at home. Each unit gets clear communication and daily cleanup.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Evanston, IL

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper usually costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Evanston, IL

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Evanston, IL. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Evanston, IL

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Evanston, IL has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. HVM Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.
