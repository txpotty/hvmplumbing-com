    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Evanston, IL

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Commercial Gas Line Installation in Evanston

    Restaurants, laundromats and light industrial spaces need gas piping sized for high-demand equipment like fryers, ovens, dryers and rooftop units. We install schedule 40 black iron or approved commercial systems, add regulators where elevated pressure is supplied and coordinate inspections. Work can be scheduled outside business hours to prevent downtime. Final documentation is provided for your landlord, insurer or fire marshal.

    Outdoor Kitchen and BBQ Gas Lines in Evanston

    Built-in grills, side burners and pizza ovens are a favorite part of backyard living. We trench from the house to the island and install yellow polyethylene gas pipe with tracer wire, transitioning to steel above grade with an approved riser. Each appliance gets an accessible shutoff valve. We bury the line to code depth and protect it where it crosses landscaping or irrigation.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in Evanston

    The cost for gas line installation in Evanston usually ranges from $500 to $2,500, depending on the length of the run, the pipe material, black iron or CSST, and the complexity of the installation. A simple hookup for a single appliance is often on the lower end, while long runs or multiple connections cost more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Gas line installation is extremely dangerous and should only be performed by licensed professionals. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, explosions, fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It also voids warranties and often fails local inspections, so it is never a DIY project.

    Common materials include black iron pipe, which is very durable, and corrugated stainless steel tubing, which offers flexibility and can be faster to install. Copper tubing is sometimes used for smaller diameter lines but is less common for main lines. Our team chooses the best material for your application and local codes.

    Materials like CSST and fittings carry manufacturer warranties, and we warranty our installation labor. We will go over the specific terms with you before work starts.

    Yes, gas line installation almost always requires a permit and inspection. We handle the permit and inspection scheduling for you.

    We use trusted, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety. This includes manufacturers like Gastite and OmegaFlex for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe and fittings from reputable suppliers. All materials are selected to meet or exceed local plumbing codes.

    Most residential gas line installations for a single appliance can be completed in a few hours to one day. More complex projects, such as extending lines for outdoor kitchens or multiple appliances, might take one to two days. This timeline does not include permitting and inspections, which can add several days to the schedule.

    CSST bonding connects the gas piping to the home's electrical grounding system to reduce damage from lightning strikes. It is a code and manufacturer requirement we follow on every job.
