    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Evanston

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. A long heating season keeps boilers and water heaters running hard, and humid summers can cause sweating pipes and damp basements. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Evanston

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system.     Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Evanston, IL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Water and sewer lines must be buried well below the frost line to avoid freezing, and shallow lines are a common source of winter trouble. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
