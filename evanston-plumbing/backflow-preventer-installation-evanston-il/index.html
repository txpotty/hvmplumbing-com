    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Evanston, IL

    Irrigation systems in Evanston, IL are a frequent source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We are experienced in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Evanston

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to keep from shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water provider.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Evanston, IL

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost a good deal more in parts and labor. Size, brand, enclosure needs and how much existing pipe must be modified all affect the price. Replacing a failed old device with a new one is usually cheaper than repeated rebuilds. We give you an itemized quote that includes the initial certification test.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Evanston

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Evanston, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Evanston, IL

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Evanston, IL

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Most Illinois homeowners shut off and drain outdoor spigots in the fall, and many keep cabinet doors open and faucets dripping on the coldest nights. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
