    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Evanston

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry serious risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes often require a licensed plumber for this work.

    HVM Plumbing installs all major reputable brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is usually required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.
