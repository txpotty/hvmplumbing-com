    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Evanston, IL

    Kitchen drains are the most common grease trouble spot in any home or restaurant. Grease clings to pipe walls and hardens, especially where the line runs flat under a slab. Jetting melts and scrubs this buildup away instead of just poking a hole through it. We use smaller hose and nozzles sized for 1-1/2 and 2 inch kitchen lines. This keeps your sink and dishwasher draining well for much longer.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Evanston

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and bring back flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond properly.

    Residential Sewer Line Jetting in Evanston

    Main sewer lines in homes are usually 3 or 4 inches and can collect years of grease, soap, mineral scale and roots.     Hydro Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Evanston

    Hard water across much of Illinois leaves mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. Even moderate mineral content can lead to scale buildup in pipes over many years. This scale can restrict flow and trap other debris, leading to clogs. Hydro jetting is highly effective at breaking down and flushing out these hard mineral deposits, restoring the full diameter of your pipes. This is a better method compared to chemical treatments for scale removal.
