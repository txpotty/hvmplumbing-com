    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Evanston

    Simple trap or slip-joint repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Replacing a section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on access. We provide a written price before starting.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises coming from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from drains and water backing up into fixtures. If you notice any of these issues, it's wise to call a professional before the problem gets worse.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also lead to early failure.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is commonly used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also seldom clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or mask a larger problem.

    Minor fixture drain repairs usually do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.

    We work with all standard drain pipe materials found in Illinois homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our repairs and replacements use high-quality, code-compliant materials from trusted manufacturers for durability and longevity.
