    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Orland Park

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an excellent option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are especially well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. HVM Plumbing carefully replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Orland Park

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives accurate readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in how well the system holds up.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Orland Park

    Properties with livestock, irrigation or more than one home on a well need more storage than a single residential tank. We install multiple tanks manifolded together or large capacity models over 100 gallons. Proper sizing keeps big three-phase pumps from cycling too often. We also check the check valves and pressure ratings on the distribution lines. Work is scheduled to keep water downtime brief for animals and occupants.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Orland Park

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.
