    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Repair in Orland Park

    Most repairs fall between $150 and $450, including valve rebuilds, injector cleaning and motor replacement. Resin replacement usually costs $400 to $900. We give a firm quote after testing.

    While water softeners are designed to be low maintenance, a professional check-up every 1 to 3 years can help prevent major breakdowns and extend the system's life. This allows technicians to inspect for wear and tear, clean components, and ensure optimal performance before problems start.

    Several factors can cause a water softener to stop working, including a clogged drain line, a faulty control valve, a salt bridge in the brine tank, depleted resin beads, power outages affecting the timer, or mineral buildup in various components. Our diagnostics will pinpoint the exact cause.

    A quality softener can last 10 to 15 years or more, though resin may need replacement sooner with chlorinated city water. Control valves usually need a seal kit after several years.

    Key signs include hard water symptoms returning (scale buildup, dry skin, dingy laundry), the softener using too much or too little salt, continuous regeneration, unusual noises, visible leaks, or the system not responding to controls. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional.

    Breaking up a salt bridge or cleaning the brine tank is a fair DIY task. Valve rebuilds, injector work and resin replacement are easier with a technician who has the right tools and parts.
