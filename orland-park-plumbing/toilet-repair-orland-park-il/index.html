    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Orland Park, IL

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Orland Park

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Orland Park, IL

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you plainly if replacement is the better fix.
