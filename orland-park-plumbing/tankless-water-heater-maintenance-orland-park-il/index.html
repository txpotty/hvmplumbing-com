    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Orland Park

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Orland Park, IL

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their robust build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to avoid damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a frequent area for attention in Orland Park.
