    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Orland Park

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Orland Park. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HVM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Orland Park

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Orland Park, IL

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or renovated.
