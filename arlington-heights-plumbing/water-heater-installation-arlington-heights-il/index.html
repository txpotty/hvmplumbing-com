    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Arlington Heights

If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a better spot.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Arlington Heights

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Plumbers in Illinois must hold a state license, so checking a license before work starts is one of the easiest ways to protect your home. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Arlington Heights. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Arlington Heights

    The cost of water heater installation in Arlington Heights, IL can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units usually have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. HVM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Plumbers in Illinois must hold a state license, so confirming a license before work starts is one of the easiest ways to protect your home. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Arlington Heights as part of our service.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    HVM Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.
