    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Arlington Heights, IL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Water and sewer lines must be buried well below the frost line to avoid freezing, and shallow lines are a common source of winter trouble. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Arlington Heights

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system. When one or more units fail, we replace them and rebalance the cascade controls so they split the load. We check gas pressure under full demand, since commercial racks can draw more than 600,000 BTU combined. Scheduling is easy to work around, so we can work after hours and keep your business open. We also set up a descaling schedule matched to your water usage.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Arlington Heights, IL

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We check your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Arlington Heights

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Illinois has cold winters and hot, humid summers, and those seasonal extremes put steady stress on pipes, water heaters, and sump pumps. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.
