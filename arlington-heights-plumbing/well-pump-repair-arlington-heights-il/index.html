    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Arlington Heights, IL

    Many well pump issues come from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Arlington Heights

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Jet Pump Repair in Arlington Heights, IL

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Arlington Heights is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.
