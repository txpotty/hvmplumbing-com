    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Arlington Heights, IL

    When repairs are no longer sufficient due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer various replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive option for your Illinois property, giving you a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Arlington Heights, IL

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Arlington Heights.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Arlington Heights, IL

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Arlington Heights.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Arlington Heights, IL

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack older sewer laterals and let tree roots find their way inside. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Arlington Heights, IL

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to serious damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. HVM Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures. We precisely locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to limit disruption.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Arlington Heights, IL

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.
