    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Arlington Heights

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet usually runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, usually around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderate water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup in toilet tanks and bowls over time, affecting performance and requiring more regular cleaning. While it doesn't directly impact installation, choosing a toilet with a good glaze and strong flush can help reduce these effects.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's usually best left to professionals. Improper installation can lead to leaks, wobbling, inefficient flushing, and potential water damage. Professional plumbers deliver proper sealing, secure mounting, and adherence to codes.

    Generally, replacing an existing toilet with a similar model in the same location in Arlington Heights does not require a permit. However, if you are relocating a toilet, adding a new toilet, or making significant changes to the plumbing system, a permit may be required. We can explain local code requirements.

    We are experienced in installing all major toilet brands, including Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and many others. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific requirements for each manufacturer to deliver a proper and efficient installation.
