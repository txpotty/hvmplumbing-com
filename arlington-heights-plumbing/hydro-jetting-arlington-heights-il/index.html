    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Arlington Heights, IL

    While snaking might be cheaper upfront for simple clogs, hydro jetting offers a more complete and long-lasting solution, often saving money in the long run for Arlington Heights, IL residents. Snaking usually punches a hole through a blockage, leaving residue behind, which can lead to recurring clogs. Hydro jetting cleans the entire pipe, preventing future issues and reducing the frequency of service calls, making it a more cost-effective choice for persistent drain problems.

    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Arlington Heights, IL

    Kitchen drains are the most common grease trouble spot in any home or restaurant. Grease clings to pipe walls and hardens, especially where the line runs flat under a slab. Jetting melts and scrubs this buildup away instead of just poking a hole through it. We use smaller hose and nozzles sized for 1-1/2 and 2 inch kitchen lines. This keeps your sink and dishwasher draining well for much longer.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in Arlington Heights

    Tree roots are a common problem in Arlington Heights, IL, especially where mature landscaping grows over older sewer lines. Roots can work into tiny cracks in pipes and grow, causing severe blockages. Hydro jetting uses a powerful stream to cut through and blast away these invasive roots. This method is far more effective than snaking for complete root removal and slowing their rapid regrowth.

    Root Cutting With Hydro Jetting in Arlington Heights

    Specialty root-cutting nozzles spin or use focused streams to slice through fine roots inside a sewer. This is useful on root hairs that a cable saw can leave behind. Roots from yard trees and shrubs often enter at clay pipe joints or cracks. Jetting clears them out and gives a clean view of each entry point on camera. That helps you decide whether lining or a spot repair is the right move.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Arlington Heights

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond correctly.
