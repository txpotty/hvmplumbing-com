    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Arlington Heights

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Arlington Heights, IL

    Noritz tankless water heaters are sturdy systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, ensuring proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for maximum efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Arlington Heights, IL

    A tankless descaling visit typically costs far less than replacing a scaled heat exchanger, which can run $600 to $1,500 or more in parts and labor. Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for. We help you decide whether a softener or scale inhibitor would reduce your long-term costs too.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Arlington Heights

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Arlington Heights homes.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Arlington Heights

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Commercial Tankless Rack Maintenance in Arlington Heights

    Restaurants, salons and multi-family buildings often run several tankless units linked together. Commercial racks see heavy use and scale faster than home units. We descale each heater, check cascade controls and balance the load across the system. Quarterly or semiannual service keeps the hot water flowing during busy hours.

    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Arlington Heights

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Arlington Heights is a smart measure against costly repairs. Our thorough check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, providing recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Arlington Heights

    Proper ventilation is critical for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Arlington Heights includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We confirm that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.
