    Main Water Line Replacement in Arlington Heights

    The main water line is the primary pipe carrying water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most cost-effective long-term solution. We carefully excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Arlington Heights

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and budget-friendly choice for water line replacement in Arlington Heights. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for simpler installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Arlington Heights

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage. If your main water line in Arlington Heights has multiple leaks, severe corrosion, or is nearing the end of its lifespan, replacement is often the more sensible long-term solution. Our technicians will perform a thorough inspection, including pressure testing and possibly video inspection, to recommend the best course of action. We consider factors like the age of your home, the material of the existing pipe, and the nature of the damage before advising.
