    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Arlington Heights, IL

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.
