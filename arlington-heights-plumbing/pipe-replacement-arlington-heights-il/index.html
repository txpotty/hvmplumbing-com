    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Arlington Heights, IL

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes.
