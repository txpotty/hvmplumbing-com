    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Arlington Heights

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Arlington Heights

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Arlington Heights, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Arlington Heights, IL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.
