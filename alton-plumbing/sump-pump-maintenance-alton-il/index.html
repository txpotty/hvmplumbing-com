    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Alton, IL

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Alton, IL.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Alton, IL

    Many Alton, IL homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages.     Submersible Sump Pump Service in Alton

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Alton due to their quiet operation and compact design.
