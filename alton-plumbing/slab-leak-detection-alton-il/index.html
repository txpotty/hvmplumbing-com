    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Alton

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Alton

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Alton homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Alton

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Alton home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Alton

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Alton, IL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.
