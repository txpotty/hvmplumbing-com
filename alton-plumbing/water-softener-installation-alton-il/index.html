    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Alton

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. HVM Plumbing expertly installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Alton home.

    Water Softener Replacement in Alton, IL

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore full performance.
