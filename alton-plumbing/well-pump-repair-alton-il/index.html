    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Alton, IL

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Backwater valves, overhead sewer conversions, and battery backup sump pumps are popular upgrades for homes with a history of flooding. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Alton, IL

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HVM Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Alton operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Alton

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Alton, IL

    Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire. If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with clear numbers.
