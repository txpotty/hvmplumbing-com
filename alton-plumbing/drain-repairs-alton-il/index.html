    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Alton

    The cost of drain repairs in Alton, IL varies widely depending on the nature and severity of the problem. Simple clog removals might range from $150 to $400, while more complex issues like sewer line repairs could be anywhere from $800 to $5,000 or more, especially if excavation is required. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after diagnosis.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also seldom clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's usually used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.

    HVM Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer a warranty on our drain repair services, which varies depending on the type of repair and parts used. Our technicians will provide details on the specific warranty that applies to your service.

    The length of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or extensive digging are involved.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more snaking.

    Repeat clogs often come from grease buildup, hard water scale, poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a damaged pipe that catches debris. A camera inspection shows which cause you are dealing with so it can be fixed for good.

    A dry trap, a cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring or a blocked vent can all let sewer gas into a room. We locate the source with a smoke test or visual inspection and seal it.
