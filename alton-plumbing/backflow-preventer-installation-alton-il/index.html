    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Alton

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Alton

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then annually. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Alton, IL

    For high-hazard applications in Alton, IL where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HVM Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes.
