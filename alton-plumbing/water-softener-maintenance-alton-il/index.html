    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Alton

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Alton, IL

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Alton, IL

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.
