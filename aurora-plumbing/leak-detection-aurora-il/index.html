    Gas Leak Detection Services in Aurora

    Gas leaks are very dangerous and require immediate professional attention.     Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Aurora, IL

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in many yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills jump in summer.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Aurora, IL

    Leaks in underground water lines, often leading from the meter to your home, can result in significant water loss and property damage. We use ground microphones and tracer gas detection to locate these buried leaks precisely. Factors like tree roots, ground shifting and corrosion in older galvanized pipes can contribute to these issues, making professional detection important.

    Slab Leak Detection in Aurora

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use dedicated acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you real repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines beneath the slab.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Aurora, IL

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to serious drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to precisely pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's interior.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Aurora

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to find temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, clean access opening.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Aurora

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, oddly green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, focused hole.
