    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Aurora

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Hard water across much of Illinois leaves mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent early failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills down.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Aurora

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Illinois has cold winters and hot, humid summers, and those seasonal extremes put steady stress on pipes, water heaters, and sump pumps. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Aurora

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Aurora, IL

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Aurora

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Aurora

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We make sure your setup meets local code.
