    Lateral and Spot Lining in Aurora

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We confirm the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Aurora

    Sewer line issues can be a nightmare, but our trenchless sewer line repair services limit the mess and disruption. We can repair or replace sewer lines damaged by tree roots, corrosion, or ground shifting. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack older sewer laterals and let tree roots find their way inside. We can handle everything from minor cracks to complete pipe collapses, restoring your sewer system promptly and efficiently in Aurora.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Aurora

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Aurora, IL

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Aurora, IL.

    Sectional Point Repair in Aurora

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Aurora without affecting the entire line.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Aurora, IL

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Pipe Bursting in Aurora, IL

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.
