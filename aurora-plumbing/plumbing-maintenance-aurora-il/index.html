    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Aurora

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack clay sewer pipes and let tree roots find their way inside.     Backflow Preventer Testing in Aurora

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Aurora

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Aurora, IL

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Aurora

    Illinois has a large stock of older homes, and many still have cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, or original clay sewer pipe.
