    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Aurora

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Illinois, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining options to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Aurora

    Before any repair, we use high-resolution video camera inspection to precisely locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This no-dig method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a clear diagnosis and repair plan. It's key for understanding issues in older homes in Aurora and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Aurora

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Aurora dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with persistent drain issues.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Aurora, IL

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to serious damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. HVM Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures. We accurately locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to minimize disruption.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Aurora

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Aurora

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Aurora

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Illinois homes.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Aurora, IL

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.
