    Soil type has a big effect on septic system performance. Much of Illinois sits on heavy clay and rich prairie soil that holds water, which puts pressure on basement walls and drain tile.     Homeowners should not open or enter septic tanks because of toxic gases. Repairs involving tanks, pumps and fields are best done by a professional with proper equipment.

    HVM Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems.
