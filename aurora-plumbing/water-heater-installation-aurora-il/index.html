    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Aurora

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater usually costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often run $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to confirm the work meets local building and safety codes. Water heater replacements, repiping, sewer line work, and adding fixtures typically require a permit and an inspection by the local authority. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Aurora as part of our service.

    HVM Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Aurora, IL, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.
