    Sink Trap and P-Trap Repair in Aurora, IL

    The P-trap under your sink is essential for preventing sewer gases from entering your home and catching items that fall down the drain. If it's leaking, corroded, or improperly installed, it can cause significant problems. We provide expert repair and replacement of P-traps, ensuring a proper seal and function for your sink plumbing in Aurora, IL.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Aurora, IL

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works smoothly. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Aurora

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. Vessel sinks can rock on a worn mounting ring, and older porcelain basins can crack around the drain. We reattach, reseal or replace the basin as needed and recaulk with a mildew-resistant silicone. We will tell you plainly when a crack means replacement is the better value.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Aurora

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of harsh chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the actual problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Aurora

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the fastest and least expensive fixes we do.
