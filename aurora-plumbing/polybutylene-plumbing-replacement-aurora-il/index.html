    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Aurora

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderately hard water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Aurora

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written replacement estimate.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Aurora

    Most PEX replacements of polybutylene cost $5,000 to $12,000 for an average home. Copper costs more, and yard service lines add $1,500 to $4,000.

    Properly installed PEX piping typically has a lifespan of 25 to 50 years, while copper piping can last 50 to 70 years or even longer. Both are major upgrades from polybutylene and offer decades of reliable service for your Aurora, IL home.

    A typical full home repipe for polybutylene plumbing usually takes between 3 to 5 days. Factors like home size, number of fixtures, and ease of access to existing pipes can affect the timeline. Our team works diligently to complete the project efficiently while maintaining quality.

    Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderate water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. While hard water doesn't directly cause polybutylene to fail like chlorine does, it can contribute to scale buildup within the pipes. This buildup can reduce water pressure and potentially worsen problems if the pipe material is already compromised.

    While there will be some disruption, HVM Plumbing strives to minimize it. We take precautions to protect your property, cover furniture, and clean up completely. We often complete the work in sections to maintain some water access during the project.
