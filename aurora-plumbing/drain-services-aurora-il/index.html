    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Aurora

    The cost of drain cleaning in Aurora can vary a great deal based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the issue.

    Traditional sewer line replacement can involve digging trenches, which can be messy. However, we also offer trenchless sewer repair options, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, which limit excavation and disruption to your Aurora property.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains, foul odors coming from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and multiple fixtures draining slowly. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call a professional before the problem escalates to a full blockage or a sewage backup.

    A camera inspection is worth it if clogs keep coming back, you are buying an older home or roots are suspected. It shows the exact location and cause of the problem.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a sag in the pipe.

    Hydro jetting is a thorough drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often needed for severe clogs, tree root intrusions, grease buildup, or when traditional snaking isn't effective, particularly for main sewer lines.

    Hydro jetting is safe for pipe in sound condition, including PVC and most cast iron. We camera the line first so we do not jet a pipe that is currently cracked or collapsing.
