    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Aurora

    Residential hydro jetting in Aurora usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and harm fragile pipe.

    The length of a hydro jetting service in Aurora varies depending on the severity and location of the clog. A typical residential drain might take 1 to 2 hours, while a major sewer line or commercial job could take 3 to 4 hours or more. Our technicians work quickly to minimize disruption.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is usually safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by HVM Plumbing is crucial to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.
