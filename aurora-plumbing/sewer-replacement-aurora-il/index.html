    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Aurora

    All sewer replacement work performed by HVM Plumbing in Aurora closely adheres to local plumbing codes and regulations. This includes proper pipe sizing, depth, slope, and connection methods.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Aurora

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is reliable for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Aurora homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Aurora, IL

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from major damage, making it a common choice for homeowners in Aurora who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll assess if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Aurora, IL

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Aurora, IL

    Many older homes in Aurora, IL still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a major upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Aurora, IL

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends ongoing root problems.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Aurora, IL

    Basements are the norm across most of Illinois, so sump pumps, floor drains, and wall seepage are common service concerns. Soil movement can cause foundation shifts, which in turn can damage sewer lines. Our replacement process in Aurora, IL takes foundation integrity into account. We plan excavations carefully and ensure proper soil compaction and backfill to prevent future issues that could impact both your new sewer line and your home's foundation. We use careful methods to protect your property.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Aurora

    Commercial buildings often have larger sewer lines and grease issues. We replace commercial laterals and work with businesses to reduce downtime. We may install grease interceptors as needed. We handle permits and city coordination. Our crews work quickly to restore service.
