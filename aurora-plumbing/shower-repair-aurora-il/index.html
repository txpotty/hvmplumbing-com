    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Aurora

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Aurora, IL

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Aurora, IL

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be incredibly frustrating. This can stem from several issues, including a failing water heater, sediment buildup in the pipes, or a problem with the shower mixing valve itself. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower. We provide effective solutions to restore consistent hot water flow and pressure.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Aurora

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Aurora, IL

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Aurora

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause serious damage if left unaddressed.     Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Aurora

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will make sure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Aurora

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders.
