Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so prompt repair is crucial.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Aurora, IL

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Aurora, IL homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs suggest a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers use professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes.     Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Aurora, IL

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve.     Running Toilet Repair in Aurora

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to significantly higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Aurora, IL

Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Repair in Aurora

    Most simple toilet repairs, like a new flapper or fill valve, run roughly $125 to $275 including parts and labor. Resetting a toilet on a new wax ring is typically $175 to $350, and flange repairs can bring the total to $300 to $500. We give you a flat price before starting.

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium deposits on flush valve seats, fill valve ports and rim jets. Over time this causes leaks, slow refills and weak flushes. Descaling and choosing parts made to resist scale help repairs last longer.

    If the porcelain is sound and the toilet flushes well, a repair is usually the smart choice. If it uses 3.5 gallons or more per flush, clogs often or has a cracked tank, replacing it with a 1.28 gallon WaterSense model often makes more sense.

    The most common causes are a worn flapper, a mineral-crusted flush valve seat or a fill valve set too high so water spills into the overflow tube. A chain that is too short can also hold the flapper open. A quick inspection usually pinpoints the cause in minutes.

    Most flapper, fill valve and supply line repairs take 20 to 45 minutes. Pulling and resetting a toilet typically takes about an hour, and flange repairs can add another 30 to 60 minutes.

    While some minor issues like a simple flapper replacement can be a DIY fix, many toilet repairs, especially those involving the internal mechanisms, drain lines, or significant leaks, are best left to professionals. Improper repairs can lead to greater damage, water leaks, or code violations.

    Generally, basic toilet repairs like replacing internal tank components, fixing a leak, or clearing a clog do not require a permit in Aurora, IL.
