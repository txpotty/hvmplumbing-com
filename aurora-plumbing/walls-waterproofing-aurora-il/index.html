    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Aurora, IL

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Newer homes and remodels mostly use copper or PEX for supply lines, while older bungalows often mix several materials. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We take care to respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Aurora

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using specialized epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques.     Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Aurora

    Newer homes and remodels mostly use copper or PEX for supply lines, while older bungalows often mix several materials. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Aurora

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Aurora, IL

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Aurora, IL

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Aurora, IL

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.
