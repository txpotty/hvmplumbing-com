    The cost of rooter service in Oak Lawn, IL usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and location of the clog.     Most standard rooter services for localized clogs can be completed in 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as main sewer line root removal or extensive hydro-jetting, may take longer, potentially 3 to 6 hours. We aim to complete the job promptly while staying thorough.

    HVM Plumbing stands by the quality of our work. We offer a warranty on our rooter services, the details of which will be provided with your estimate. This gives you confidence knowing that our solutions are designed to last.

    We use industry-leading professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and durability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at HVM Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes. Our technicians are skilled in operating the equipment correctly to clear blockages without causing damage. However, if pipes are already compromised, such as corroded or cracked, some methods may worsen existing issues, which is why a camera inspection is often recommended first.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and foul odors coming from drains. If you notice any of these issues, especially multiple fixtures backing up, it's a clear sign you need professional rooter service.
