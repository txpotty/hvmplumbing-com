    Expansion Tank Replacement in Oak Lawn, IL

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Expansion Tank Installation Cost Comparison in Oak Lawn, IL

    Adding an expansion tank during a water heater replacement is the most cost-effective approach because the water is already off and the piping is open. Installing one as a standalone job costs a bit more in labor but still pays for itself by protecting the heater. Larger tanks, attic installs and PRV replacement add to the total.
