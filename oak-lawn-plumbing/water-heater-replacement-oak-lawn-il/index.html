    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Oak Lawn

    The cost of water heater replacement in Oak Lawn, IL typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and necessary plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the appropriate GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Oak Lawn homes.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderate water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best left to licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options around your household and budget.

    Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.
