    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Oak Lawn, IL

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance.     Sump Pump Maintenance in Oak Lawn, IL

    Heavy spring and summer storms often overwhelm older sewer systems in Illinois, causing sewage backups through floor drains. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Oak Lawn.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Oak Lawn

    Illinois has a large stock of older homes, and many still have cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, or original clay sewer pipe. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.
