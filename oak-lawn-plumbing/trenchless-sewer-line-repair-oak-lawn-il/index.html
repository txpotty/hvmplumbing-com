    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Oak Lawn, IL

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Oak Lawn

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Oak Lawn homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
