    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Skokie

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Skokie, IL

    Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack clay sewer pipes and let tree roots find their way inside. Soil movement can cause foundation shifts, which in turn can damage sewer lines. Our replacement process in Skokie, IL takes foundation integrity into account. We plan excavations carefully and ensure proper soil compaction and backfill to prevent future issues that could impact both your new sewer line and your home's foundation. We use careful methods to protect your property.
