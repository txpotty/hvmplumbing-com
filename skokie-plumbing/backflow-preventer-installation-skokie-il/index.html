    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Skokie

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Skokie, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Skokie

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Skokie, IL

    Irrigation systems in Skokie, IL are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Skokie

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Skokie. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition.
