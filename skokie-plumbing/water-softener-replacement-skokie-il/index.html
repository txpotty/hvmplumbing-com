    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Skokie

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderately hard water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Skokie homes.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.

    HVM Plumbing installs a wide range of trusted water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a high-quality system that meets your needs.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is generally not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be required. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That determines the resin capacity needed.
