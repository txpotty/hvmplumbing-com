    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Skokie

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    Most well pumps last 8 to 15 years. Sediment, hard water and frequent cycling can cut lifespan.

    Yes, we often replace well pressure tanks alongside well pumps or as a standalone service. A properly functioning pressure tank is crucial for pump longevity and consistent water pressure. We assess your entire well system so all components run together efficiently.

    New well pumps usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. HVM Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    Most well pump replacements are completed in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.
