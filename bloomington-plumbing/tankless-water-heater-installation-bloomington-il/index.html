    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Bloomington

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line often needs to be upsized.
