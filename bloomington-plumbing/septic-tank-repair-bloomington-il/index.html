    Frequently Asked Questions About Septic Tank Repair in Bloomington

    Homeowners should not open or enter septic tanks because of toxic gases. Repairs involving tanks, pumps and fields are best done by a professional with proper equipment.

    HVM Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems. Our technicians are experienced with various septic system designs, from conventional gravity systems to aerobic treatment units.

    Frequent causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria.     The duration of septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue.     If your septic tank is overflowing, immediately stop all water use in your home to prevent further spillage.
