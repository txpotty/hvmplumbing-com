    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bloomington

    Many homes in Bloomington use natural gas for their water heaters due to its efficiency and lower operating costs. Our licensed plumbers are experts in gas appliance installation, ensuring every safety measure is met.     Expansion Tank Installation in Bloomington, IL

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Bloomington

    The cost of water heater installation in Bloomington, IL can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units generally have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Illinois has its own statewide plumbing code administered by the state health department, and some larger cities enforce their own codes as well. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Bloomington as part of our service.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by gallons per minute.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Bloomington, IL, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is usually required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.
