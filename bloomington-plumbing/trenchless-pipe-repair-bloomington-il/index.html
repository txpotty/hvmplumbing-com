    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Bloomington, IL

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HVM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Bloomington, IL.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Bloomington, IL

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Bloomington, IL.
