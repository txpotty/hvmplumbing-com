    Complete Water Softener Maintenance Services in Decatur, IL

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Decatur

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain plainly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Decatur, IL

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Decatur homes.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Decatur, IL

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Decatur, IL

    A maintenance visit typically costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed. Resin rebedding typically costs $300 to $700 and can bring an older tank back to full capacity. Most quality softeners last 10 to 15 years with service. We will tell you when a repair makes sense and when a replacement is the smarter investment.
