    Slab Leak Detection in Decatur

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HVM Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Decatur, IL

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Decatur

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste large amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore efficient watering for your landscape.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Decatur, IL

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.
