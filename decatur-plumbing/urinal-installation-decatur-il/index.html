    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Decatur

    The cost for urinal installation in Decatur, IL can range from $400 to $1,200, depending on the type of urinal, complexity of plumbing modifications, and whether it's a new installation or a replacement. This price usually includes labor and basic materials, but highly specialized units or extensive rough-in work can increase the overall cost.

    While it might seem like a way to save money, urinal installation involves precise plumbing connections, proper venting, and secure mounting that are best handled by a professional. Incorrect installation can lead to leaks, odors, and expensive repairs down the line, making professional service a worthwhile investment.

    For commercial settings, look for durability, water efficiency (like low-flow or waterless models), easy maintenance, and ADA compliance if applicable. Brands like Sloan and Zurn are popular for their sturdy commercial-grade fixtures and flushometers.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more forcefully down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    Switching from a 1 gpf urinal to a 0.125 gpf model saves most of the water per flush. In a busy restroom that adds up to tens of thousands of gallons a year.
