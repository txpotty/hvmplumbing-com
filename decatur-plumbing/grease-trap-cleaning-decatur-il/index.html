    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Decatur

    The cost of grease trap cleaning in Decatur, IL varies based on the size of the trap, its accessibility, and the amount of accumulated FOG. Typically, prices can range from $200 for smaller under-sink units to over $1,000 for large outdoor interceptors. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after assessing your specific needs.

    The terms are often used interchangeably, but 'grease interceptor' typically refers to larger, outdoor units with capacities over 500 gallons, while 'grease trap' usually describes smaller, indoor units under 100 gallons, such as under-sink models. Both serve the same function of separating FOG from wastewater.

    Grease trap cleaning removes fats, oils, and grease (FOG), along with food solids, sediment, and wastewater. This material is often referred to as 'brown grease' and requires specialized handling and disposal to prevent environmental contamination.

    The duration of a grease trap cleaning can vary, but most standard commercial cleanings take between 1 to 3 hours. Larger outdoor interceptors or traps with significant buildup may take longer. We aim to complete the service efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations in Decatur.

    Yes, we provide a service record after every cleaning. It includes the date, volume removed and condition of the trap, which you can show the inspector.

    HVM Plumbing services all common types of grease traps and interceptors, including passive hydromechanical grease interceptors (HGIs), automatic grease removal units (AGRUs), and large gravity grease interceptors (GGIs). We handle both indoor under-sink units and outdoor in-ground tanks.

    FOG stands for Fats, Oils, and Grease. When poured down drains, FOG cools and solidifies, sticking to pipe walls and eventually leading to severe clogs. In grease traps, it separates from wastewater, but if not regularly removed, it overflows into the sewer system, causing blockages and environmental issues.
