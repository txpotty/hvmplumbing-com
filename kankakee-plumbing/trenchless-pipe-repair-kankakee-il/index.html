    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Kankakee

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Sectional Point Repair in Kankakee

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an effective trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and limits the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Kankakee without affecting the entire line.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Kankakee, IL

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Kankakee, IL

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HVM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Kankakee, IL.
