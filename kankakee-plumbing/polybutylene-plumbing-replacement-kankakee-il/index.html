    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Homes supplied by lake water tend to have moderate water, while most of Illinois relies on groundwater that runs hard to very hard.
