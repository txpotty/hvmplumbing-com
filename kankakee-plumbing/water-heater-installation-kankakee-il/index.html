    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Kankakee

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. HVM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help lengthen life.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.
