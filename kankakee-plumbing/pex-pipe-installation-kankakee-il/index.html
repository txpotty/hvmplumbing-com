    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Kankakee

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Kankakee, IL

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Kankakee

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Kankakee can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
