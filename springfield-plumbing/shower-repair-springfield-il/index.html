    Shower Drain Repair in Springfield

    Slow shower drains are usually clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components when needed.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Springfield

    A shower that drips after it is turned off typically has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Springfield

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model. We offer options from standard to rain showerheads, ensuring proper installation for strong performance and water conservation.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Springfield

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Springfield, IL

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.
