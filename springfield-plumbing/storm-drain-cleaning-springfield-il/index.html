    Catch Basin Cleaning in Springfield, IL

    Catch basins are critical parts of any storm drainage system in Springfield, IL, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is essential to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to heading off larger issues.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Springfield, IL

    Hydro jetting is a powerful solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Springfield, IL. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is highly effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Springfield, IL

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

This non-invasive diagnostic tool gives accurate problem identification and lets us provide the most effective and targeted cleaning or repair solutions.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Springfield

    The cost of storm drain cleaning in Springfield usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and type of blockage, the length of the drain, and the equipment required, such as hydro jetting or snaking. More complex issues like extensive root intrusion or collapsed pipes may cost more. We provide a clear, upfront estimate before starting any work.

    While you can remove surface debris from drain grates, deeply clogged storm drains require professional tools like hydro jetters and camera equipment for effective cleaning. DIY attempts can sometimes push debris further down or damage the pipes, leading to more costly repairs. It is safer and more effective to hire a professional.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is very effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    To prevent storm drain clogs, regularly clear leaves and debris from grates and catch basins, avoid planting trees directly over drain lines, and keep landscaping in good shape to prevent soil erosion. Consider installing grates with smaller openings in high-debris areas and schedule yearly professional cleanings.

    Common signs include water pooling around your property after rain, slow drainage from downspouts, visible debris collecting at catch basin grates, and unusual odors coming from the drains. If you notice any of these, it is a good idea to have your storm drains inspected.

    In Springfield, common causes of storm drain clogs include built-up leaves, dirt, and organic debris from trees, silt and sediment from soil erosion, trash, and sadly, sometimes even illegally dumped waste. Tree root intrusion into the pipes is also a major factor, especially in older systems.

    We use a range of professional-grade equipment, including heavy-duty hydro jetting machines that use high-pressure water to scour pipes, drain snakes with various cutting heads for root removal, and waterproof camera inspection systems to accurately diagnose issues and confirm thorough cleaning.

    Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems typically found in residential and commercial properties. This includes standard PVC, corrugated HDPE, concrete, and cast iron drainpipes, as well as various types of catch basins and grates from manufacturers like NDS, ADS, and Zurn.
