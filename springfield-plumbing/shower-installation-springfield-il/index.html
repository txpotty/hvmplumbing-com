    Steam Shower Plumbing in Springfield

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Springfield

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Springfield, IL

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and ensures consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Springfield.
