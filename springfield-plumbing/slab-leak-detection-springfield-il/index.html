    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Springfield, IL

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can appear as wet spots or high water bills in your Springfield, IL home. We have separate methods for each.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Springfield, IL

    Slab leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. Direct access repairs usually run $1,000 to $3,000, including concrete patching but not flooring replacement. Reroutes generally cost $800 to $3,500 depending on distance and access. Full repipes cost more, often $4,000 to $15,000, but end the problem for good.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Springfield, IL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Leak Detection for Older Illinois Homes in Springfield, IL

    Many older homes in Springfield, IL have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more prone to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points. We provide careful analysis for the challenges presented by vintage plumbing under concrete slabs.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Springfield

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Springfield

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Springfield homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.
