    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Springfield

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Springfield

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with spot repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in Springfield will conduct a complete camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution. We provide clear explanations and upfront pricing for both options.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Springfield

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Springfield, IL

    Many older homes in Springfield, IL still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.
