    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so periodic testing is important.

    To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates. Annually, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from HVM Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Springfield home.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how often it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are extensive plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without sacrificing quality.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space.
