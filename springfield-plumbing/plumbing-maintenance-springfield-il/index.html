    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Springfield

    Lead service lines remain in many older neighborhoods, and state rules now require utilities to replace them over time. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.
