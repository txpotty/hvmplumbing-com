    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Springfield

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Springfield

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Springfield, IL

    A sudden sump pump failure can lead to immediate flooding, especially during heavy rainfall. HVM Plumbing offers emergency sump pump replacement in Springfield, IL to address urgent situations.     Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Springfield

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Springfield for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Springfield

    Backwater valves, overhead sewer conversions, and battery backup sump pumps are popular upgrades for homes with a history of flooding. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Springfield provides an important layer of protection. We add a secondary, battery-powered pump that instantly activates if the primary power fails, keeping flood protection running even without electricity. This is a vital upgrade for many homes.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Springfield

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Springfield, IL
