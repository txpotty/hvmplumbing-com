    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Springfield, IL

    Leaks at pipe joints are often due to improper soldering, fatigued connections or shifting foundations.     Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Springfield

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize over time. Hard water minerals build up inside valves and prevent them from closing. We replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that are easier to operate. That makes future repairs and emergencies easier to handle.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Springfield

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur odors, and sediment are frequent reasons homeowners call for treatment or repairs. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which might show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues. We make sure all new connections are properly sealed and protected.
