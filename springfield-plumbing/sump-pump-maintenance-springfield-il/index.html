    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Springfield

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch.     Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Springfield

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Springfield

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Springfield residence.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Springfield, IL

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.
