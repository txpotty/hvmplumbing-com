    Pressure Switch & Gauge Integration in Springfield, IL

    A well pressure tank replacement often involves inspecting and, if necessary, replacing the associated pressure switch and pressure gauge. These components are critical for regulating your well pump's operation and monitoring system pressure. Our plumbers in Springfield, IL ensure all these elements work together smoothly for a fully functional and efficient well water system.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Springfield, IL

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Springfield

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the usable water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the best ways to extend pump life.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Springfield, IL

    While our primary service is well pressure tank replacement, HVM Plumbing also stresses the importance of preventative maintenance for your well system in Springfield, IL. Regular checks of your pressure tank's air charge and overall condition can help identify potential issues before they lead to a complete failure. During a maintenance visit, we can top off the air charge, inspect for early signs of corrosion or leaks, and advise on steps to extend the lifespan of your current tank, potentially delaying the need for replacement.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Springfield

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Our technicians in Springfield provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.
