    Copper Pipe Replacement in Springfield, IL

    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. HVM Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Springfield, IL.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Springfield, IL

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Springfield

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.
