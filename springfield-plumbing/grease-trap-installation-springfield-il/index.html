    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Springfield

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Springfield, IL

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks usually range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Clay soil shifts as it wets and dries, which can crack clay sewer pipes and let tree roots find their way inside. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Springfield, IL

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an avoidable expense. Our plumbers carefully calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, ensuring optimal performance for your Springfield, IL business.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Springfield, IL
