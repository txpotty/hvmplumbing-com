    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Springfield

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for efficient, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Springfield

    Many older homes in Springfield still have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials resist corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Springfield, IL

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Springfield and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Springfield

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Springfield

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.
