    Most homeowner's insurance policies cover water damage from sudden and accidental events, like a burst pipe or water heater leak.     HVM Plumbing stands behind our flood restoration work with a comprehensive warranty on our repairs and services.     The duration of flood restoration varies based on the severity of the damage.     Drywall and flooring replacement usually do not need permits, but water heater replacement, electrical repairs and structural work often do. We handle plumbing permits when needed.

    Water heaters whose burners, controls or insulation were submerged are often unsafe and should be replaced. We inspect affected units and recommend repair or replacement. Gas appliances should never be relit until they are inspected.
