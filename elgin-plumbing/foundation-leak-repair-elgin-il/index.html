    Professional Foundation Leak Repair Services in Elgin, IL

    Foundation Leak Detection in Elgin

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Elgin, IL

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Water Line Reroute in Elgin

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Elgin

    Modern homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or poor installation. HVM Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use correct fusion and solvent welding techniques to make strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Elgin foundation.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Elgin, IL

    Crawl space foundations can suffer leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not addressed quickly. Our team thoroughly inspects the crawl space and identifies the source of water intrusion. We then put in place solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Elgin, IL from future moisture issues.
