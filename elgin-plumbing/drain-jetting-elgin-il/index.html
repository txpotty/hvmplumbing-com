    Laundry Drain Jetting in Elgin, IL

    Washing machines discharge lint, fabric softener residue and fine sand or grit. These build into a gray sludge that slows the standpipe and overflows during the spin cycle. Jetting clears the standpipe and branch line completely. We also check the trap, vent and drain size to make sure they can handle the washer flow.     Kitchen Drain Jetting in Elgin

    Kitchen lines are where grease, food particles and dish soap collect and harden. Over time the pipe narrows until the sink drains slowly or backs up into the other bowl. We access the line through a cleanout or the trap arm and feed a small jetting hose toward the main. Rear-facing jets scrub the walls while the stream flushes residue away. Your sink and dishwasher drain at full speed once more.
