    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Naperville

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until serious damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to accurately pinpoint these hidden leaks in Naperville, minimizing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service efficiently.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Naperville

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Naperville looking to preserve their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Naperville

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are experienced in repairing both. PEX offers flexibility and corrosion resistance, while copper is known for its durability. We carry a full stock of matching fittings and tools for each material, ensuring a proper, code-compliant repair. Our expertise extends to identifying common failure points for each type, such as fitting issues with PEX or pinhole leaks in older copper lines, common in homes with harder water.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Naperville

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Naperville

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.
