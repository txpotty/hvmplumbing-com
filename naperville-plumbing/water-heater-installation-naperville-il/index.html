    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Naperville

    The cost of water heater installation in Naperville, IL typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Water heater replacements, repiping, sewer line work, and adding fixtures typically require a permit and an inspection by the local authority. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Naperville as part of our service.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes often require a licensed plumber for this work.

    Yes, we stand behind our work. Our water heater installations come with a labor warranty, and the water heater unit itself will have a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model.

    The typical lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all influence the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.
