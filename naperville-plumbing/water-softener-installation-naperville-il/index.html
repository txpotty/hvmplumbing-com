    Softener Loop Installation in Naperville

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Naperville

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. HVM Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Naperville home.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Naperville

    Accurate testing is key for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Naperville

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing healthy minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an effective option for scale prevention and are popular in many Naperville households.
