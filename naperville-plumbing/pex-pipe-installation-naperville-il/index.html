    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Naperville

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Naperville can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Naperville, IL

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.
