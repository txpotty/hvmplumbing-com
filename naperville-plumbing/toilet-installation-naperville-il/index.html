    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Naperville

    The cost of toilet installation in Naperville, IL typically ranges from $200 to $500, not including the toilet fixture itself. This price can vary based on factors like the type of toilet, any necessary plumbing modifications, or the difficulty of removing an old unit.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and simpler cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, usually around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location generally does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    Common signs that you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, constant running, visible cracks in the bowl or tank, excessive wobbling, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. If you're constantly repairing it, replacement is often more cost-effective.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, usually takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, may take longer.
