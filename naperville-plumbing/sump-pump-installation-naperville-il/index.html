    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    A well-maintained sump pump typically lasts about 7 to 10 years. However, its lifespan can vary based on usage frequency, water quality and pump quality. Regular inspections by a professional can help determine if your sump pump in Naperville is nearing the end of its useful life.

    To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to ensure it activates. Once a year, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch.
