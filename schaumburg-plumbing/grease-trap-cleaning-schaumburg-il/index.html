    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Schaumburg

    Small indoor grease traps usually cost $150 to $400 per cleaning. Large outdoor interceptors often run $300 to $1,200 depending on size and buildup. Regular maintenance plans can lower the cost per visit.

    HVM Plumbing services all common types of grease traps and interceptors, including passive hydromechanical grease interceptors (HGIs), automatic grease removal units (AGRUs), and large gravity grease interceptors (GGIs). We handle both indoor under-sink units and outdoor in-ground tanks.

    The frequency of grease trap cleaning depends heavily on usage. Commercial kitchens often require cleaning every 1 to 3 months, while residential units might only need it once a year or less. Local Schaumburg health codes for restaurants might mandate specific intervals.

    The duration of a grease trap cleaning can vary, but most standard commercial cleanings take between 1 to 3 hours. Larger outdoor interceptors or traps with significant buildup may take longer. We aim to complete the service efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations in Schaumburg.

    Yes, our technicians are familiar with all major grease trap brands and manufacturers commonly found in Illinois, including but not limited to Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. We can service, clean, and often repair components for these systems.

    Staff can sometimes clean very small under-sink traps, but waste must be disposed of properly. Outdoor interceptors require a vacuum truck and a licensed hauler, and many cities require professional records.

    The terms are often used interchangeably, but 'grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor units with capacities over 500 gallons, while 'grease trap' usually describes smaller, indoor units under 100 gallons, such as under-sink models.
