    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Schaumburg, IL

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to inconsistent water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these crucial components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Schaumburg, IL

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Schaumburg

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Schaumburg, IL

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We give you an honest assessment of the repair options for your Schaumburg, IL home.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Schaumburg

    A common culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a malfunctioning igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to make for a quick fix and restore your hot water supply promptly.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Schaumburg, IL

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Schaumburg

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Pilot Light Repair in Schaumburg

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.
