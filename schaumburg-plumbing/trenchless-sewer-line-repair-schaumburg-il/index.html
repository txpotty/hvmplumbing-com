    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Schaumburg

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Schaumburg, IL

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Schaumburg

    Before any trenchless repair, we conduct a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This crucial step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Schaumburg property, avoiding guesswork.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Schaumburg

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Schaumburg that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Schaumburg

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Schaumburg homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
