    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Schaumburg, IL

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Schaumburg, IL

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We prioritize non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Schaumburg, IL from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and reduces the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Schaumburg, IL

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Schaumburg

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Schaumburg homes.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Schaumburg

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.
