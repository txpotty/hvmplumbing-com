    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Tinley Park

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Tinley Park residence.

    Emergency Sump Pump Maintenance in Tinley Park

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Tinley Park, IL

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Tinley Park

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a regular basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Tinley Park

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Tinley Park, IL

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Tinley Park

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Tinley Park due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, avoiding electrical hazards and supporting longevity.
