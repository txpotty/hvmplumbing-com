    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Tinley Park, IL

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Tinley Park, IL

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.
