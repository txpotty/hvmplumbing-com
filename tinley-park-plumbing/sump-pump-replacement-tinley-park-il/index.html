    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Tinley Park

    Heavy spring and summer storms often overwhelm older sewer systems in Illinois, causing basement backups through floor drains.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Tinley Park

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Tinley Park

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of effective models, from entry-level to professional-grade. If your Wayne sump pump is failing, HVM Plumbing provides fast Wayne sump pump replacement in Tinley Park.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Tinley Park, IL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Tinley Park, IL, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.
