    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Joliet, IL

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Hard water across much of Illinois leaves mineral scale in water heaters, faucets, and appliances, so softeners are a common upgrade. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Joliet, IL, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Joliet

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Joliet, IL

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition.     Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Joliet
