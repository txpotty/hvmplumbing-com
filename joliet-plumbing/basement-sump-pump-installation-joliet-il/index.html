    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Joliet

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. HVM Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your Joliet home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Joliet, IL

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that switch on automatically if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Joliet, IL. Heavy spring and summer storms often overwhelm older sewer systems in Illinois, causing sewage backups through floor drains. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Joliet

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.
