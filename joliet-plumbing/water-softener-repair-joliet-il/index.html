    Softener Not Regenerating in Joliet

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage.     Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Joliet

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention.     Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Joliet, IL

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair usually wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Joliet, IL

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can effectively break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Joliet, IL

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses specialized tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform careful repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Joliet, IL

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. HVM Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these specialized units in Joliet, IL, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Joliet, IL

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Joliet, IL

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.
