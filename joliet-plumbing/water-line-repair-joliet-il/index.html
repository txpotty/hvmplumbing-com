    A spot repair on an accessible service line typically runs $350 to $1,500 depending on depth and access. Full replacement from meter to house usually costs $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often falls between $3,000 and $7,000. We give you a written price before starting.

    For water line replacements, we primarily use durable and code-compliant materials such as PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper. PEX is flexible and resistant to freezing, while copper offers excellent longevity and corrosion resistance. The best material for your home in Joliet will depend on your particular needs, budget, and local regulations.

    Many policies do not cover the pipe itself but may cover resulting damage to your home. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their utility or insurer. We provide photos and an itemized invoice to back up your claim.

    Most standard water line repairs can be completed within a few hours to a full day, depending on the severity and accessibility of the leak.     For significant water line repairs or replacements, particularly on the main service line connected to the city supply, a plumbing permit is often required in Joliet. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so your repair complies with local regulations and passes inspection.     Preventative measures include regular plumbing inspections, being mindful of tree roots near your water lines, and protecting outdoor pipes from extreme temperatures. For older homes with galvanized pipes, considering a full replacement can prevent ongoing issues. We can advise on preventative steps suited to your property.

    Trenchless water line repair is a method of fixing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging.
