    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet.     Condensing Tankless Replacement in Downers Grove

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Illinois has cold winters and hot, humid summers, and those seasonal extremes put steady stress on pipes, water heaters, and sump pumps. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Downers Grove, IL

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use in check while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is a favorite in long single-story ranch homes.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Downers Grove, IL

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Most Illinois homeowners shut off and drain outdoor spigots in the fall, and many keep cabinet doors open and faucets dripping on the coldest nights. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
