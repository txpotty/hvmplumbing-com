    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Downers Grove

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Private wells serve many rural homes, and iron, sulfur odors, and grit are frequent reasons homeowners call for treatment or repairs. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which may show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures.     Commercial Copper Pipe Repair in Downers Grove

    Many commercial buildings use larger diameter copper for domestic water supply. Leaks in these systems can affect multiple tenants and require careful shutdown planning.     Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Downers Grove

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize over time. Hard water minerals build up inside valves and prevent them from closing.     Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Downers Grove

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Downers Grove, IL

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside. We repair leaking hot lines and can adjust or add timers to your recirculation system. That reduces wear and lowers energy use.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Downers Grove, IL

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original workmanship, thermal expansion or vibration. A leaking joint usually shows a white or green crust around the fitting. We clean, reheat and resolder the joint or replace the fitting entirely if it is corroded. The result is a clean, fully sweated connection that holds pressure.
