    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Downers Grove, IL

    Many well pump issues stem from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults.     Jet Pump System Repair in Downers Grove, IL

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HVM Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Downers Grove operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Downers Grove

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.
