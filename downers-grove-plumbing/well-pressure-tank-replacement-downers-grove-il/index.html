    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Downers Grove, IL

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below. We replace corroded diaphragm tanks and check the stand and fittings under them. The new tank is leveled and supported so it does not stress the piping.     Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Downers Grove

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and choke flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives accurate readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in reliability.

    Rapid pump cycling, pressure that swings up and down, and water coming out of the air valve are typical signs. A tank that sounds solid and full when tapped near the top is often waterlogged.
