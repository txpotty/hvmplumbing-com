    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Leak Repair in Downers Grove

    The cost of gas leak repair in Downers Grove, IL can vary a lot depending on the leak's location, severity and the extent of pipe damage. Minor repairs, like tightening a connection, might range from $150 to $400, while more complex issues involving pipe replacement could be between $500 and $2,500 or more. We provide a clear estimate after a thorough inspection.

    A carbon monoxide detector does not detect natural gas leaks. Natural gas has no smell of its own, so an additive gives it a rotten egg odor, while carbon monoxide is an odorless, colorless gas that results from incomplete combustion. While a carbon monoxide detector is essential for safety, it won't alert you to a gas leak. You need to rely on your sense of smell or a separate gas leak detector.

    Many gas line repairs and replacements require a permit and inspection, especially when new pipe is installed. We take care of permits when required.

    Most areas have no annual requirement for homeowner gas line inspections, but it is highly recommended to have your gas lines inspected every 3 to 5 years, or whenever you notice a potential issue like an odd smell or a drop in appliance performance. Regular inspections can identify problems before they become dangerous leaks, especially where ground movement or tree roots are a concern.

    The length of a gas leak repair varies greatly. Simple repairs might take an hour or two, while complex issues involving excavation or extensive pipe replacement could take a full day or more. We aim for prompt service while putting safety and thoroughness first.

    The most common signs of a gas leak include a distinct smell of rotten eggs or sulfur, a hissing or whistling sound near a gas line, visible damage to a gas pipe or dead vegetation over an underground gas line. Indoor symptoms might also include dizziness, nausea or fatigue. If you notice any of these, evacuate and call for help immediately.
