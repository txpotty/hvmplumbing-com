    Drop-In Sink Installation in Downers Grove, IL

    Drop-in or self-rimming sinks are the most forgiving style and a good choice for laminate counters. We check the cutout, seal the rim with silicone or plumber's putty as the maker specifies, and connect a new pop-up drain. This is often the quickest and most affordable sink installation. It is also a simple way to refresh an older vanity.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Downers Grove, IL

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter, which changes the drain height and faucet requirements. We install the mounting ring, set the bowl level, and use a vessel-specific drain with or without an overflow as the basin requires. We also confirm your faucet is a tall or wall-mount model that reaches past the rim. The trap arm is adjusted so everything lines up under the counter.

Our plumbers ensure the drain and supply lines are perfectly aligned within the pedestal column and securely anchored to the wall. This prevents wobbling and keeps all connections hidden and watertight. We handle the delicate nature of ceramic pedestals with care, providing a stable and attractive fixture for your home in Downers Grove, IL.
