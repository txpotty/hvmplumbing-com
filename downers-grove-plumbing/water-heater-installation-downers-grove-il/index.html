Plumbers in Illinois must hold a state license, so checking a license before work starts is one of the easiest ways to protect your home. HVM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Downers Grove as part of our service.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years.     The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand.     Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours.
